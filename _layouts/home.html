The five-minute triage that returns four gigabytes.

A step you can run before coffee. Open the built-in task manager, sort by memory footprint, and be surprised who's winning.

  1. Press Shift + Esc (Windows) or Search + Esc (Mac) to open the task manager.
  2. Enable Memory Saver at chrome://settings/performance and restart.
  3. Flip Proactive Tab Freeze and Discard in chrome://flags.
  4. Exempt your email and two work apps so they don't get discarded.
  5. Audit extensions — three are usually eating more than your tabs.
