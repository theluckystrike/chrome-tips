## Key Differences

### Active Writing Assistance vs Lesson Review

BeLikeNative integrates artificial intelligence for text paraphrasing, rewriting, and translation tasks. You can highlight text on any webpage and get alternative phrasings, improved clarity, or translation into different languages. This functionality supports content creation, academic writing, and professional communication across language barriers.

Busuu Lesson Review focuses specifically on reviewing language lessons within the Busuu learning platform. It does not provide general writing assistance or AI-powered text manipulation outside its specific lesson review context.

Busuu Lesson Review operates exclusively within Busuu's learning environment. Its functionality does not extend to general web browsing or writing tasks outside the lesson review system.

**How is Busuu different from BeLikeNative for language learning?**
BeLikeNative is an AI writing assistant that works on all websites, helping you write better in real-time. Busuu Lesson Review is a companion extension that only works within the Busuu learning platform to enhance structured lessons. They serve different purposes and audiences.

**Does BeLikeNative offer structured courses like Busuu?**
No. BeLikeNative is an on-demand writing tool, not a structured language course. It improves text you have already written through paraphrasing, translation, and rewriting. For curriculum-based language learning with lessons and progress tracking, Busuu provides the structured framework.

**Is Busuu better for learning grammar than BeLikeNative?**
Yes. Busuu includes grammar lessons, exercises, and native speaker feedback as part of its curriculum. BeLikeNative focuses on improving text quality but does not teach grammar rules systematically.

**Which is better for everyday browser use: BeLikeNative or Busuu?**
BeLikeNative is far more useful for everyday browser use because it works on every website. Busuu Lesson Review only activates within the Busuu platform. For anyone who writes emails, documents, or social media posts regularly, BeLikeNative provides immediate daily value.
