## Key Differences That Matter

### AI Writing Capabilities

**BeLikeNative** transforms how you work with text beyond simple translation. The AI paraphrasing feature helps you rewrite sentences for different audiences, while the rewriting tool adjusts tone and complexity. When tested with technical documentation, BeLikeNative made dense content accessible to non-technical readers in seconds.

iTranslate sticks to its core mission: language conversion. You get accurate translations but zero writing assistance. For basic language needs, that works fine. For content creation or professional communication, BeLikeNative delivers substantially more value.

### Performance Impact

The 999KiB size of BeLikeNative makes it one of the lightest translation extensions available. iTranslate requires more system resources, especially when handling voice features or maintaining offline language packs. For users running multiple productivity extensions simultaneously, this difference is noticeable.

### Language Coverage vs. Feature Depth

iTranslate supports over 100 languages including rare dialects and regional variants. BeLikeNative covers 50+ major languages but focuses on quality over quantity. If you work with Swahili, Welsh, or other specialized languages, iTranslate provides better coverage. For mainstream languages, both perform well.

The real difference lies in what happens after translation. BeLikeNative lets you refine, rewrite, and optimize translated content. iTranslate delivers the translation and stops there.

> "The tested Chrome translation extensions in 2025 show a clear split between tools built for basic language conversion and those designed around broader writing workflows." — [The Tested 6 Best Chrome Translation Extensions in 2025](https://www.swifdoo.com/blog/chrome-translation-extension), swifdoo.com

### Integration Depth

BeLikeNative integrates naturally with Chrome's context menu and text selection workflows. Right-click any text to access paraphrasing, translation, or rewriting options. The extension adapts to your writing style over time.

iTranslate requires more manual interaction. You typically need to open the extension popup, paste text, select languages, and copy results back. This workflow slows down when processing multiple pieces of content throughout a workday.

## When to Choose Each Extension

**Choose BeLikeNative if:**
- You create content regularly and need writing assistance beyond translation
- System performance matters and you prefer lightweight extensions
- You work primarily with major world languages

**Choose iTranslate if:**
- You need voice translation capabilities for audio content
- Your work involves rare languages or regional dialects
- Offline translation is essential for your workflow
- You prefer specialized tools that do one thing extremely well

Both extensions handle basic translation tasks competently. The choice depends on whether you view translation as a standalone need or part of a broader writing workflow.

## When BeLikeNative Isn't Enough

BeLikeNative falls short in three specific scenarios. Professional linguists working with rare language pairs will find the 50+ language limit restrictive. iTranslate's extensive language database serves specialized translation needs better.

Voice translation presents another gap. If you regularly translate audio content, podcasts, or spoken conversations, iTranslate's voice features prove essential. BeLikeNative focuses on text-based workflows exclusively.

Offline functionality matters for international travelers or users in areas with unreliable internet. iTranslate downloads language packs for offline use, while BeLikeNative requires an internet connection for AI processing.
