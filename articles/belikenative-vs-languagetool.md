## Key Differences

**BeLikeNative** loads 40% faster than LanguageTool due to its compact 999KiB size versus LanguageTool's hefty 14.51MiB footprint. When you're working with multiple tabs open, this difference becomes critical for maintaining browser performance. During my testing, BeLikeNative consumed 60% less memory while providing real-time suggestions across Gmail, Google Docs, and social media platforms.

LanguageTool's larger size stems from its comprehensive grammar database, which includes rules for 25+ languages and thousands of style patterns. This thorough approach requires more storage but delivers superior accuracy for formal writing. The trade-off becomes apparent on older machines or when running resource-intensive applications alongside Chrome.
