## Key Differences

### Reading Aid vs Writing Tool

Perapera is designed for one specific use case: reading Japanese and Chinese text in the browser. When you hover over a character or word, it displays readings, definitions, and in the case of Japanese, furigana above kanji. This is an indispensable tool for learners and working professionals reading native East Asian content online.

BeLikeNative does not offer character-level lookup. It can translate selected text blocks from Japanese or Chinese to English, but it does not provide the granular, hover-activated word-by-word lookup that makes Perapera useful for active reading practice.

> "Hover-based dictionary extensions for Japanese and Chinese significantly reduce reading friction for learners by providing immediate character readings without disrupting the reading flow." — [Lingopie, Best Chrome Extensions for Language Learning](https://lingopie.com/blog/best-chrome-extensions-for-language-learning/)

### Translation Approach

BeLikeNative translates passages and paragraphs, useful for getting the gist of a foreign article or for writing assistance across languages. You highlight a block of text and receive a translation.

Perapera operates at the word and character level. It tells you what individual characters mean and how to read them, which is a fundamentally different interaction model. For someone learning Japanese and working through a native article, Perapera's word-level approach builds comprehension skills. BeLikeNative's block translation provides understanding but bypasses the learning step.

### Language Breadth

BeLikeNative covers many major world languages for general translation and writing assistance. Perapera specializes in Japanese and Chinese, which limits its usefulness for users working with other languages.

For users working primarily with European or other major languages, BeLikeNative's broader coverage is more practical. For anyone doing serious work with Japanese or Chinese, Perapera's specialization is unmatched by a general-purpose writing assistant.

> "Specialized Chrome extensions that target specific language pairs or scripts provide a level of depth that general-purpose translation tools cannot replicate, particularly for logographic writing systems." — [Noiz, 7 Best Chrome Translation Extensions: Comparison](https://noiz.io/blog/the-7-best-translation-extensions-for-chrome/)

## When to Choose Each

Choose **BeLikeNative** if you:
- Write content and need AI assistance to improve or translate it
- Work with multiple languages beyond Japanese and Chinese
- Want paraphrasing and rewriting tools alongside translation
- Need a general-purpose writing companion for professional work

Choose **Perapera** if you:
- Regularly read Japanese or Chinese text in the browser
- Want hover-based furigana and pinyin for reading practice
- Are learning Japanese or Chinese and need character-level help
- Need a lightweight, focused tool for East Asian reading

## Can You Use Both?

Yes. These tools serve genuinely different functions and do not conflict. A Japanese learner who also writes professional content in English would benefit from running both: Perapera for reading Japanese articles and BeLikeNative for writing assistance and translation of longer passages.

## The Verdict

BeLikeNative and Perapera do not compete in any meaningful way. The choice depends entirely on your language needs. For East Asian language reading, Perapera is a dedicated specialist. For writing assistance and general translation, BeLikeNative is the stronger everyday tool.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is Perapera and what languages does it support?**
Perapera is a Chrome extension that provides hover-activated readings and definitions for Japanese and Chinese characters. When you move your cursor over a Japanese kanji or Chinese character, it shows the reading (furigana for Japanese, pinyin for Chinese) and definition. It supports Japanese and Chinese primarily.

**Does Perapera show furigana and pinyin on hover?**
Yes. This is Perapera's core feature. Hovering over Japanese text displays furigana above kanji characters and provides definitions. For Chinese text, it shows pinyin romanization and definitions.

**Can BeLikeNative translate Japanese characters like Perapera?**
BeLikeNative can translate selected Japanese text blocks into English and other languages. It does not provide character-level hover lookup or furigana display. The two tools use different interaction models for different use cases.

**Is Perapera free to use?**
Yes. Perapera is a free Chrome extension with no paid tier required for its core hover-based dictionary functionality.
