## 1. BeLikeNative — AI writing assistant that makes English sound natural

BeLikeNative transforms awkward ESL writing into fluent, native-sounding English through AI-powered paraphrasing and rewriting. Unlike basic grammar checkers, it understands context and cultural nuances to suggest improvements that actually sound like native speakers wrote them.

**Key features:**
- AI paraphrasing that maintains original meaning while improving flow
- Real-time translation with context preservation  
- Writing style suggestions for academic, business, and casual contexts
- Integrated vocabulary enhancement with synonym recommendations

**Price:** Free tier available, premium plans start at $9.99/month  
**Best for:** Intermediate to advanced ESL students writing essays, emails, and professional documents

*Limitation: The free version has daily usage limits that may restrict heavy users.*

## 2. Grammarly — Comprehensive grammar and style checking

Grammarly remains the gold standard for catching grammar mistakes, spelling errors, and style issues. Its ESL-specific features help non-native speakers understand why certain constructions are incorrect and suggest contextually appropriate alternatives.

**Key features:**
- Advanced grammar checking with explanations
- Tone detection and adjustment suggestions
- Plagiarism detection for academic writing
- Browser-wide integration across websites

**Price:** Free version available, premium at $12/month  
**Best for:** Students who need detailed grammatical feedback and explanations

## 3. Google Translate — Instant translation with pronunciation guides

Google Translate's Chrome extension provides immediate translation support directly in your browser. The recent addition of pronunciation guides and example sentences makes it particularly valuable for ESL students learning proper pronunciation and usage.

**Key features:**
- Instant webpage translation with highlighted text
- Audio pronunciation for proper speaking practice
- Camera translation for images and documents
- Offline translation capabilities for 59 languages

**Price:** Completely free  
**Best for:** Beginning ESL students who need frequent translation support

## 4. Language Reactor — Learn English through Netflix and YouTube

Language Reactor transforms passive video watching into active language learning by adding dual subtitles, word definitions, and pronunciation tools to streaming platforms. It's particularly effective for improving listening comprehension and learning colloquial expressions.

**Key features:**
- Dual subtitles in native language and English
- Click-to-define vocabulary with pronunciation
- Automatic pause on unknown words
- Progress tracking and vocabulary lists

**Price:** Free with premium features at $5/month  
**Best for:** Students who learn best through visual media and want to improve listening skills

## 5. Readlang — Vocabulary building through real content

Readlang allows students to click any word or phrase for instant translation while reading online articles, creating personalized vocabulary lists from authentic content. This contextual approach helps students learn words they'll actually encounter in real-world reading.

**Key features:**
- One-click word translation while reading
- Automatic flashcard creation from clicked words
- Spaced repetition system for vocabulary retention
- Progress tracking across different difficulty levels

**Price:** Free tier with limitations, premium at $5/month  
**Best for:** Students focused on expanding vocabulary through authentic reading materials

## 6. Lingvist — Adaptive vocabulary training

Lingvist uses machine learning to personalize vocabulary lessons based on individual progress and learning patterns. Its Chrome extension provides quick vocabulary practice sessions during browser breaks.

**Key features:**
- AI-powered personalized learning paths
- Quick 5-minute practice sessions
- Progress analytics and learning insights
- Integration with popular language learning methods

**Price:** Free trial, then $9.99/month  
**Best for:** Students who want structured vocabulary building with measurable progress

## 7. Rememberry — Flashcard creation from web content

Rememberry transforms any highlighted text into flashcards for later study, making it easy to save and review new vocabulary encountered while browsing. Its spaced repetition algorithm ensures optimal retention timing.

**Key features:**
- Instant flashcard creation from highlighted text
- Spaced repetition scheduling for optimal review
- Cross-device synchronization
- Multiple study modes including typing practice

**Price:** Free with premium features at $3.99/month  
**Best for:** Students who prefer traditional flashcard-based memorization techniques
