# Best Chrome Extensions for Amazon Prime Video

If you are searching for the best chrome extensions for amazon prime video, you probably want to get more out of your streaming experience. Amazon Prime Video offers a massive library of movies, TV shows, and original content, but like any streaming platform, it has some limitations that can be frustrating. The good news is that Chrome extensions can help fill in the gaps and make your viewing experience much better.

## Why Consider Extensions for Amazon Prime Video

Amazon Prime Video has become one of the most popular streaming services available. With a subscription, you get access to thousands of hours of entertainment, including exclusive original series like The Boys, The Marvelous Mrs. Maisel, and many more. However, despite all that content, there are some features that users commonly wish were built into the platform.

For instance, you might want better control over subtitles, or you might find that keeping multiple streaming tabs open causes your browser to slow down significantly. Perhaps you want to watch with friends who are far away, or you need a way to keep track of what you have been watching. These are all common frustrations that many Amazon Prime Video users experience, and they are exactly the kinds of issues that Chrome extensions can help solve.

The Chrome Web Store has a growing selection of extensions designed specifically for Prime Video users. These tools can enhance your viewing experience in ways that the standard Amazon interface does not support.

## Prime Video Picture-in-Picture

One of the most requested features for Prime Video users is picture-in-picture mode. While some browsers support this natively, the Prime Video Picture-in-Picture extension makes it easier to use. This extension allows you to pop out the video player so you can watch your show in a small window while browsing other websites or working on your computer.

This is incredibly useful when you want to keep an eye on a movie or show while checking your email, browsing social media, or doing other tasks. Instead of switching back and forth between tabs, you can have your Prime Video content playing in a small corner of your screen. The extension is simple to use and works reliably with most Prime Video content.

## Super Prime Video

Super Prime Video is a comprehensive extension that adds several useful features to your Amazon Prime Video experience. One of its most helpful functions is the ability to customize subtitles. You can change the font size, color, and background to make text easier to read. If you find the default subtitles too small or hard to see, this extension lets you adjust them to your exact preferences.

The extension also offers keyboard shortcuts for common actions like skipping forward or backward, adjusting volume, and toggling fullscreen. These shortcuts can make your viewing experience much smoother, especially if you prefer not to use your mouse while watching. Additionally, Super Prime Video can help you bypass certain regional restrictions by providing alternative streaming links when available.

## IMDb Rating for Prime Video

When you are browsing through the extensive Prime Video catalog, it can be difficult to know whether a particular show or movie is worth watching. The IMDb Rating for Prime Video extension solves this problem by displaying IMDb scores directly on the Prime Video interface.

This extension overlays ratings on thumbnails and detail pages, so you can quickly see how other viewers have rated the content. This saves you time and helps you avoid starting something that might not match your tastes. The extension also highlights Amazon Originals more clearly, making it easier to discover exclusive content that you might otherwise miss.

## StreamFab Prime Video Downloader

One common frustration with Prime Video is the limitation on offline viewing. While Amazon does allow downloads on mobile devices, many users want to download content to watch on their computers. The StreamFab Prime Video Downloader extension helps with this by providing a way to save Prime Video content for offline viewing.

This extension is particularly useful for travelers or anyone who does not always have a reliable internet connection. You can download movies or episodes to watch later when you are offline. You should only download content that you have the legal right to download for personal use. The extension provides clear instructions on how to use this feature effectively.

## Tab Suspender Pro

Tab Suspender Pro is not specifically designed for Amazon Prime Video, but it is incredibly useful for anyone who likes to keep multiple streaming tabs open. If you are someone who watches Prime Video while also browsing other websites, or if you like to have several shows open at once to switch between them, you might have noticed that this can slow down your browser significantly.

Tab Suspender Pro helps by automatically suspending tabs that you have not used recently. When you open a Prime Video tab and then switch to another tab or application, Tab Suspender Pro can pause that tab to free up system resources. This means your browser stays fast and responsive even when you have multiple streaming tabs open.

The extension works quietly in the background, and you can whitelist Prime Video so that it only suspends tabs when you want it to. This gives you the best of both worlds: a fast browser when you need it and the ability to keep your Prime Video tabs running when you are actively watching. Many Prime Video users find that this extension makes a noticeable difference in their overall browsing experience.

## Amazon Prime Video Watch Party

If you enjoy watching Amazon Prime Video with friends and family who are far away, the Watch Party extension is a great option. This extension allows you to sync your Prime Video viewing with others, so everyone watches the same thing at the same time.

Watch Party adds a chat sidebar where you can discuss what is happening in the show or movie in real-time. This makes it feel like you are together in the same room, even when you are miles apart. You can also control the playback together, which is useful if one person is in charge of choosing what to watch. This is perfect for long-distance relationships, friends who moved away, or family members who live in different cities.

## Finding the Right Extensions for Your Needs

The best chrome extensions for amazon prime video are the ones that address your specific needs. You might not need every extension mentioned here, so take some time to think about what frustrates you most about watching Prime Video. Do you want to watch in a smaller window while working? Try the picture-in-picture extension. Do you want to watch with friends remotely? Watch Party is the answer. Does your browser slow down when you have too many tabs open? Tab Suspender Pro can help.

The beauty of these extensions is that they are easy to install and remove if they do not work for you. You can experiment with different combinations to find the perfect setup that makes your Prime Video viewing more enjoyable. Small improvements in your streaming experience can make a big difference in how much you enjoy your favorite shows and movies.

Once you start using these extensions, you will wonder how you ever watched Prime Video without them. Whether it is better subtitles, easier downloads, or a faster browser, there is an extension that can help. Take some time to explore these options and customize your Prime Video experience to suit your preferences.
