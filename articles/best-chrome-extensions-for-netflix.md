# Best Chrome Extensions for Netflix

If you are searching for the best chrome extensions for Netflix, you probably want to get more out of your streaming experience. Netflix is already an amazing service, but the right extensions can make it even better by adding features that are not available out of the box. Whether you want better subtitles, offline viewing help, or a smoother streaming experience, there are extensions that can help.

## The Netflix Experience Can Be Even Better

Netflix has become the go-to streaming service for millions of people around the world. With thousands of movies and TV shows available at the click of a button, it is no wonder that so many people spend hours watching their favorite content on the platform. However, despite all that Netflix offers, there are some limitations that can frustrate users.

For example, you might find that the subtitles do not always match your preferences, or you might wish you could download shows to watch offline. Perhaps you have noticed that keeping multiple Netflix tabs open slows down your browser, or maybe you want to track which shows you have watched across different profiles. These are common pain points that many Netflix users experience, and they are exactly the kinds of issues that Chrome extensions can address.

The good news is that there is a growing ecosystem of extensions designed specifically for Netflix users. These tools can enhance your viewing experience in ways that the standard Netflix interface does not support. Here are some of the best chrome extensions for Netflix that you can start using today.

## Netflix Originals and Rating Extender

One popular extension that Netflix users appreciate is Netflix Originals and Rating Extender. This simple but useful tool adds additional information to the Netflix interface that helps you make better viewing decisions. When you are browsing through the endless catalog, it can be hard to know whether a show or movie is worth your time.

This extension displays IMDB ratings directly on the Netflix thumbnails and detail pages. Instead of guessing whether a show is good based on the thumbnail or description, you can see at a glance what other users thought of it. This saves time and helps you avoid starting something that you might not enjoy. The extension also highlights Netflix Originals more clearly, making it easier to discover content that is exclusive to the platform.

## Super Netflix

Super Netflix is one of the most comprehensive extensions for enhancing your Netflix experience. It comes with a range of features that address several common frustrations that users have with the platform.

One of the most useful features is the ability to customize subtitles. You can change the font size, color, and background opacity to make subtitles easier to read. If you find the default subtitles too small or hard to see, this extension lets you adjust them to your liking. This is especially helpful when watching foreign language films or shows with dialogue that is difficult to hear.

The extension also allows you to skip intros automatically. Many TV shows have long intro sequences that you have seen hundreds of times. With Super Netflix, you can set the extension to automatically skip these intros, saving you a few seconds each episode that adds up over time. You can also disable the previews that play when you pause or start watching, which is useful if you want to avoid spoilers or just want a cleaner viewing experience.

## Netflix Video Downloader Helper

One of the biggest limitations of Netflix is that you cannot easily download shows to watch offline unless you have the mobile app. However, there are extensions that can help with this. Netflix Video Downloader Helper is designed to assist with downloading Netflix content for offline viewing.

You should only download content that you have the legal right to download for personal use. This extension is primarily useful for users who want to watch Netflix on devices that do not have the official app, such as certain computers or tablets. It provides a way to get your Netflix content offline, which is valuable for travelers or anyone who does not always have a reliable internet connection.

The extension works by capturing the video stream and saving it in a format that you can play later. The process is straightforward, and the extension provides clear instructions on how to use it. Keep in mind that the quality of the download may vary, and the process takes some time depending on the length of the content.

## Tab Suspender Pro

While Tab Suspender Pro is not specifically designed for Netflix, it is incredibly useful for anyone who likes to keep multiple streaming tabs open. If you are someone who watches Netflix while also browsing other websites, or if you like to have several shows open at once to switch between them, you might have noticed that this can slow down your browser significantly.

Tab Suspender Pro helps by automatically suspending tabs that you have not used recently. When you open a Netflix tab and then switch to another tab or application, Tab Suspender Pro can pause that tab to free up system resources. This means your browser stays fast and responsive even when you have multiple streaming tabs open.

The extension works quietly in the background, and you can whitelist Netflix so that it only suspends tabs when you want it to. This gives you the best of both worlds: a fast browser when you need it and the ability to keep your Netflix tabs running when you are actively watching. Many Netflix users find that this extension makes a noticeable difference in their overall browsing experience.

## Netflix Party

If you enjoy watching Netflix with friends and family who are far away, Netflix Party is a game-changer. This extension allows you to sync your Netflix viewing with others, so everyone watches the same thing at the same time.

Netflix Party adds a chat sidebar where you can discuss what is happening in the show or movie in real-time. This makes it feel like you are together in the same room, even when you are miles apart. You can also control the playback together, which is useful if one person is in charge of choosing what to watch.

This extension is perfect for long-distance relationships, friends who moved away, or family members who live in different cities. It brings the social aspect of watching movies and TV shows back into your remote interactions. Setting up a Netflix Party is easy, and you can share the link with anyone who has the extension installed.

## Making the Most of Your Netflix Experience

The best chrome extensions for Netflix are the ones that address your specific needs. You might not need every extension mentioned here, so take some time to think about what frustrates you most about watching Netflix. Are the subtitles too small? Try Super Netflix. Do you want to watch with friends remotely? Netflix Party is the answer. Does your browser slow down when you have too many tabs open? Tab Suspender Pro can help.

The beauty of these extensions is that they are easy to install and remove if they do not work for you. You can experiment with different combinations to find the perfect setup that makes your Netflix viewing more enjoyable. Small improvements in your streaming experience can make a big difference in how much you enjoy your favorite shows and movies.

Once you start using these extensions, you will wonder how you ever watched Netflix without them. Whether it is better subtitles, easier downloads, or a faster browser, there is an extension that can help. Take some time to explore these options and customize your Netflix experience to suit your preferences.
