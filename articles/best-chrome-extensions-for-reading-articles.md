# Best Chrome Extensions for Reading Articles

If you are searching for the best chrome extensions for reading articles, you probably know how frustrating it can be to read online with constant distractions, cluttered layouts, and eye strain. Many Chrome users struggle to focus on article content because of ads, popups, and busy web designs that make reading difficult. The good news is that there are simple extensions that can transform your reading experience.

## Why Reading Online Can Be Frustrating

Modern websites are built to capture attention and generate ad revenue, not to deliver content comfortably. When you visit an article page, you often have to deal with advertisements that jump around, popups that block your view, and sidebars filled with distracting links. The actual article text might be squeezed into a narrow column or displayed in a font that is hard on the eyes.

The problem is not that the content is difficult. The problem is that websites make reading unnecessarily hard. You might start an article with good intentions, only to get distracted by flashing banners or give up because the text is too small or the background is too bright. This is why so many people are looking for the best chrome extensions for reading articles.

## How Reading Extensions Can Help

Chrome extensions can clean up web pages and make them more comfortable to read. They work in different ways. Some remove all the clutter and show you only the article text. Others change the colors and fonts to reduce eye strain. A few help you understand what you are reading by providing definitions or translating foreign words.

The best chrome extensions for reading articles share a few traits. They are simple to use, they work on most websites, and they let you customize the appearance to match your preferences. You do not need technical knowledge to install or use them. Once set up, they work quietly in the background making your reading experience better.

## Mercury Reader

Mercury Reader is one of the most popular extensions for improving readability in Chrome. When you click its icon, it removes ads, navigation menus, sidebars, and other distracting elements from the page. What remains is clean article text that you can read without fighting for your attention.

Beyond removing clutter, Mercury Reader lets you customize how the article looks. You can choose from different font styles, adjust the text size, and pick a background color that feels comfortable to you. Some readers prefer a light cream background, while others like a dark mode for late-night reading. The choice is yours, and you can change it anytime.

The extension also offers a keyboard shortcut, so you can clean up a page with a quick press of buttons. This is handy when you want to focus on an article quickly without clicking through menus.

## Tab Suspender Pro

One extension that helps your reading experience in an unexpected way is Tab Suspender Pro. While it is best known for saving memory by pausing tabs that you are not using, it also makes your browser run faster. When you have many tabs open, Chrome can become sluggish, which interrupts your reading flow.

Tab Suspender Pro automatically suspends tabs that you have not looked at recently. This frees up your computer's resources so the tab you are reading stays responsive. You do not have to manually manage anything. The extension works quietly in the background, and when you click on a suspended tab, it reloads instantly.

This might seem like a small thing, but a slow browser can be frustrating enough to make you give up on reading an article. By keeping Chrome running smoothly, Tab Suspender Pro lets you read without interruptions or delays.

## Google Dictionary

Sometimes you encounter words that you do not understand while reading. In the past, you might have opened a new tab to search for the definition, which breaks your concentration. Google Dictionary solves this problem by letting you look up words without leaving the page.

Simply double-click on any word, and a small popup appears with its definition. You can also click and drag to select a phrase and see its meaning. The extension supports many languages, so it is helpful if you read articles from international sources.

This tool is especially useful for students, researchers, or anyone who reads technical content. You can keep your focus on the article while still understanding unfamiliar words.

## Dark Reader

Many people read articles late at night or in rooms with dim lighting. Bright screens can cause eye strain and make it hard to fall asleep afterward. Dark Reader addresses this by inverting the colors on web pages, creating a dark theme that is easier on your eyes.

The extension does more than just flip colors. It intelligently adjusts text and background colors to maintain good contrast while reducing brightness. You can tweak the settings to change the contrast, brightness, and color saturation. Some users prefer a pure black background, while others like a dark gray that is softer on the eyes.

Dark Reader works on most websites automatically, so you do not have to configure it for each page you visit. If you read articles in the evening, this extension can make a big difference in your comfort.

## Textise

Textise takes a different approach to improving readability. Instead of just cleaning up the page, it extracts only the text content and displays it in a clean, simple format. This removes all images, videos, ads, and other media that might distract you from the main content.

The extension creates a streamlined reading view that looks like a digital book. You can adjust the text size, line height, and font to create your ideal reading environment. Textise is particularly useful when you want to quickly scan an article or when the original page design is so cluttered that it interferes with comprehension.

Some users prefer this plain text approach because it strips away everything except the words. This can help you absorb information more quickly without visual noise pulling your attention away.

## Finding What Works for You

The best chrome extensions for reading articles are the ones that match your specific needs. If you mainly struggle with distracting ads and cluttered layouts, Mercury Reader or Textise will help. If you read in different lighting conditions, Dark Reader is a game changer. If you often encounter unfamiliar words, Google Dictionary is invaluable. And if a slow browser is your issue, Tab Suspender Pro keeps everything running smoothly.

You do not need to install every extension mentioned here. Start with one or two that address your biggest frustration, and see how they improve your reading experience. You might find that just one or two tools are enough to make reading online enjoyable again.

Once you have the right extensions in place, you will be surprised at how much more you can read and how much less tired your eyes feel. The internet is full of great articles, and with these tools, you can finally enjoy them without frustration.
