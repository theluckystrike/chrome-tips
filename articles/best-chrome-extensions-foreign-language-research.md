## 2. Google Translate — Universal translation with instant results

Google Translate remains the go-to extension for quick translations across 130+ languages. The extension detects languages automatically and provides instant translations directly in your browser, making it invaluable for rapid content scanning during research.

**Key features:**
• Instant translation of selected text without page navigation
• Camera translation for images and documents
• Offline translation for 59 languages
• Voice input and pronunciation guides

**Price:** Completely free  
**Best for:** Researchers needing quick translations across multiple languages

## 3. Linguee — Context-rich translations with real examples

Linguee excels at providing translations with real-world context examples from bilingual texts. This extension is particularly valuable for researchers who need to understand how terms are used in professional and academic contexts rather than just literal translations.

**Key features:**
• Extensive database of bilingual example sentences
• Professional and technical terminology focus
• Source verification for translation examples
• Integration with major dictionaries and glossaries

**Price:** Free with optional Pro features at $4.99/month  
**Best for:** Academic researchers studying terminology in context

## 4. DeepL Translator — Premium AI translation quality

DeepL has earned recognition for producing more natural-sounding translations than traditional machine translation tools. The extension leverages advanced neural networks to provide translations that better capture nuance and context.

• Translation history and glossary management

**Price:** Free tier with 500,000 character limit, Pro plans from $8.74/month  
**Best for:** Researchers working with complex academic or technical texts

LanguageTool provides comprehensive grammar, style, and spell-checking across 20+ languages. For foreign language researchers, it's essential for ensuring accuracy when writing in non-native languages or proofreading translated content.
