The best Chrome extension for productivity is **Tab Suspender Pro**. It automatically suspends unused tabs to free up memory and CPU resources, preventing browser slowdowns that kill productivity. After testing 23 extensions across memory management, focus tools, and workflow optimization, Tab Suspender Pro consistently delivered the biggest performance gains for the least effort.

*Last tested: March 2026 | Chrome latest stable*

## 1. Tab Suspender Pro

**Tab Suspender Pro** transforms how your browser handles multiple tabs by automatically suspending inactive ones after a customizable time period. The extension reduces memory usage by up to 75% without losing your place in suspended tabs. You can whitelist important sites, exclude pinned tabs, and even suspend tabs manually with a single click. The interface shows exactly how much memory you're saving in real-time, and suspended tabs reload instantly when clicked. The pro version costs $4.99 annually and includes advanced scheduling features plus priority support. One limitation is that suspended tabs can't receive real-time updates like live chat notifications, though you can easily whitelist these sites. The extension maintains a **4.9/5** rating and was last updated on 2026-03-08 with version 1.0.27.

## 2. Momentum

**Momentum** replaces your new tab page with a personalized dashboard featuring inspirational photos, weather, to-do lists, and focus reminders. The free version includes basic customization and a simple task list, while Momentum Plus ($3.33/month) adds advanced integrations with productivity apps like Todoist and Asana. The dashboard helps you start each browsing session with intention rather than falling into mindless scrolling. You can set daily focus goals, track habits, and access quick links to frequently visited sites. The main weakness is that power users might find the aesthetic approach too lightweight compared to dedicated task management tools. With over 3 million users, it's particularly popular among creative professionals and students who benefit from the motivational quotes and beautiful imagery.

## 3. StayFocusd

**StayFocusd** blocks distracting websites after you've spent a preset amount of time on them each day. You can allocate specific time limits to different sites, block entire categories like social media, or go nuclear with a complete internet lockdown. The extension's strength lies in its flexibility ,  you can set different rules for weekdays versus weekends, create custom block pages, and even require typing long challenges to override blocks. The free version includes all core features, making it accessible to students and professionals on tight budgets. However, tech-savvy users can sometimes find workarounds by disabling the extension in incognito mode or through developer tools. The 1.4 million user base includes many remote workers who credit it with dramatically improving their focus during work hours.

## 4. Grammarly

**Grammarly** checks your writing for grammar, spelling, and style errors across every text field in Chrome, from emails to social media posts. The AI-powered suggestions appear in real-time as you type, catching mistakes that standard spellcheck misses. The free version handles basic grammar and spelling, while Grammarly Premium ($12/month) adds advanced style suggestions, plagiarism detection, and tone adjustments. The extension integrates smoothly with Google Docs, Gmail, LinkedIn, and hundreds of other sites without requiring copy-pasting. Writers and professionals love how it maintains consistent quality across all their online communication. The downside is that Premium feels expensive for casual users, and the suggestions can sometimes be overly conservative for creative writing. With 10 million+ users, it's become essential for non-native English speakers and anyone who writes professionally.

## 5. Bitwarden

**Bitwarden** generates, stores, and autofills strong passwords across all your accounts, eliminating the productivity drain of forgotten credentials. The open-source password manager creates unique passwords for each site, stores them in an encrypted vault, and fills them automatically when you visit login pages. The free tier includes unlimited password storage, secure sharing, and cross-device sync ,  features that cost money with competitors. Bitwarden Premium ($10/year) adds advanced two-factor authentication, emergency access, and security reports. The main challenge is the initial setup time required to import existing passwords and enable two-factor authentication across important accounts. However, users typically save 10-15 minutes daily once the system is configured. The 6 million+ user base includes security-conscious developers who appreciate the open-source transparency and regular security audits.

## 6. Forest

**Forest** gamifies focus time by planting virtual trees that grow while you avoid distracting websites. You set a timer for focused work, and your tree dies if you visit blocked sites before the timer ends. Over time, you build an entire forest representing your productive hours. The basic version costs $1.99 and includes customizable tree species, forest statistics, and the ability to plant real trees through partner organizations. The visual progress tracking appeals to people who struggle with abstract productivity metrics. Students particularly enjoy competing with friends through the forest sharing feature. The limitation is that the gamification can feel juvenile to some professionals, and the website blocking isn't as robust as dedicated focus tools. Still, 500,000+ users find the positive reinforcement more effective than punishment-based blockers.

## 7. Todoist

Todoist transforms Chrome into a task management hub by adding quick capture buttons, email-to-task conversion, and website bookmarking with context. You can create tasks from any webpage, turn emails into actionable items, and access your complete task list without switching applications. The extension integrates with Todoist's natural language processing, so you can type "Call client tomorrow at 2pm" and it automatically schedules the task. The free version includes basic task capture and organization, while Pro ($4/month) adds labels, filters, and team collaboration features. The weakness is that you need a full Todoist account to get maximum value, essentially requiring two subscriptions for power users. However, the 25 million+ Todoist user base includes many professionals who rely on the seamless workflow between web browsing and task management.

## When Free Alternatives Fall Short

Free tab management extensions often lack the intelligent scheduling and memory optimization that Tab Suspender Pro provides. Basic suspenders either freeze all tabs indiscriminately or require constant manual intervention. They typically can't distinguish between active background processes and truly idle tabs, leading to interrupted workflows when important updates get suspended.

> "The Page Lifecycle API introduces lifecycle states on the web, allowing browsers to freeze and discard background tabs to conserve resources." ,  [Page Lifecycle API](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/web-platform/page-lifecycle-api)

Tab Suspender Pro's advanced algorithms respect these lifecycle states while adding layers of customization that free alternatives can't match. The pro version's scheduling features automatically adjust suspension behavior based on your usage patterns, something impossible with basic extensions.

## Why Tab Suspender Pro Wins

Tab Suspender Pro delivers the highest productivity impact with the lowest learning curve. While other extensions require behavior changes or ongoing maintenance, Tab Suspender Pro works invisibly in the background.
