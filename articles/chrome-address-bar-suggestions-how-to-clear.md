# Chrome Address Bar Suggestions How to Clear

If you are searching for chrome address bar suggestions how to clear, you have probably noticed that Chrome keeps suggesting websites, searches, and even partial URLs as you type in the address bar. While these suggestions can be convenient, they can also feel intrusive, show things you would rather not see, or reveal your browsing history to others using your computer. The good news is that clearing these suggestions is straightforward, and I will walk you through exactly how to do it.

## Why Chrome Address Bar Suggestions Appear

Chrome shows suggestions in the address bar based on several factors. The most obvious source is your browsing history. Every website you visit gets recorded, and when you start typing in the address bar, Chrome matches what you type against your history and shows the most relevant matches. This is helpful when you visit a site frequently and want to get back quickly, but it can be embarrassing if someone else is using your computer and your browsing history pops up unexpectedly.

Beyond your history, Chrome also uses data from your bookmarks, recent downloads, and even popular websites from the web. When you enable sync, Chrome can also pull suggestions from your Google account across all your devices. This means that suggestions you see are not just from the current computer but from your entire browsing ecosystem.

Another factor many people do not realize is that websites can inject suggestions into Chrome through the Address Bar API. This allows sites to suggest their own URLs as you type, which can be useful for navigation within a site but also means companies can promote their content directly in your address bar.

The combination of these sources means your address bar can quickly fill up with suggestions you did not ask for, including old sites you visited once and forgot about, bookmarks you no longer need, and suggestions from websites trying to drive traffic to their pages.

## How to Clear Address Bar Suggestions

The quickest way to clear individual suggestions is to hover over the suggestion you want to remove and then press Shift and Delete on your keyboard. This removes that specific suggestion from your history. However, this only works one at a time and does not clear everything.

To clear all address bar suggestions at once, start by clicking inside the address bar to make sure it is active. On Windows, press Ctrl, Shift, and Delete all at the same time. On Mac, press Command, Shift, and Delete. This opens the Clear browsing data window.

In this window, make sure the checkbox next to "Browsing history" is checked. You might also want to check "Cookies and other site data" and "Cached images and files" depending on how thorough you want to be, but for just clearing address bar suggestions, the browsing history option is what matters.

Next, look at the dropdown menu that says "Time range." Click on it and select "All time" if you want to wipe everything. This is the most complete option and will remove all suggestions from your history. If you only want to remove recent suggestions, you can choose a shorter time period like "Last hour" or "Last week."

When you are ready, click the blue button that says "Clear data." Chrome will take a moment to process, and when it finishes, your address bar suggestions will be gone. The next time you type in the address bar, it will start fresh with only new suggestions based on your future browsing.

## How to Stop Suggestions from Appearing

If you find address bar suggestions annoying and want to prevent them from showing up in the future, Chrome offers a few settings to help. Open Chrome and click the three dots in the upper right corner, then select Settings. In the settings page, look for the section called "Privacy and security" on the left sidebar and click on it.

Within Privacy and security, click on "Sync and Google services." On this page, you will see an option called "Autocomplete searches and URLs." Click on it and you can choose between three options. "Always" shows suggestions from browsing history, bookmarks, and the web. "Only for suggestions from Google" limits suggestions to search queries sent to Google. "Never" turns off address bar suggestions entirely.

If you choose "Never," Chrome will still let you type in the address bar, but it will not show any dropdown suggestions as you type. You will need to press Enter to perform a search or navigate to a URL manually.

Another useful setting is to manage what data Chrome uses for suggestions. Go back to the Privacy and security section and click on "Clear browsing data." Below the clear button, you will see a link that says "Cookie and site permissions." Click on it to manage permissions for specific websites. If certain sites are adding unwanted suggestions, you can block them from storing data, which will prevent them from appearing in your address bar.
