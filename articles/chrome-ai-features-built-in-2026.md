One of the most useful chrome ai features built in 2026 is the enhanced tab management system. If you often find yourself with dozens of open tabs, you will appreciate how Chrome now automatically suggests ways to organize them. The AI analyzes your open tabs and creates logical groups based on the content you are viewing. Chrome can now provide concise summaries of web page content, helping you quickly decide whether a page is worth your time.
