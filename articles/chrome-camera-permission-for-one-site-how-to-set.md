# Chrome Camera Permission for One Site How to Set

You want to use your camera for a video call on one specific website in Chrome, but you are not sure how to give just that one site permission without affecting others. If you have been searching for chrome camera permission for one site how to set, this guide will walk you through exactly what you need to do. Chrome makes it pretty straightforward to control camera access for individual websites, and you will be able to set it up in just a few minutes.

## Why Site-Specific Camera Permissions Matter

When you allow camera access for one website, you might not want that same permission to apply to every other site you visit. Some websites genuinely need your camera for their core features, like video calling apps, online meeting platforms, or virtual event sites. Other websites might ask for camera access but do not really need it. By setting permissions on a site-by-site basis, you keep control over your privacy and only allow camera access where it makes sense.

Chrome remembers your choices for each website, so you do not have to keep granting permission every time you visit. This is convenient, but it also means you should check your permissions every now and then to make sure you are not giving more access than you intended.

## Setting Camera Permission for One Specific Site

The easiest way to set camera permission for one site in Chrome is through the address bar. Here is how to do it step by step.

First, open the website where you want to allow or block camera access. Look at the left side of the address bar at the top of your Chrome window. You will see a lock icon, a warning icon, or some other symbol depending on the site. This icon represents the permissions for the current website.

Click on that icon. A small menu will appear showing you what permissions this website currently has. Look for "Camera" in the list. You will see a dropdown or a toggle next to it showing whether the camera is currently allowed, blocked, or set to ask each time.

Click on the camera setting and change it to your preferred option. You can choose to allow the camera, block it, or set Chrome to ask you each time the site tries to use the camera. Once you make your choice, the setting is saved immediately for that specific website.

If you do not see the camera option in the address bar menu, it means the website has not asked for camera access yet. The menu will only show permissions that the site has actually requested at some point.

## Managing All Site Permissions in One Place

If you want to see all the websites that have camera permission or you want to make changes to multiple sites at once, Chrome has a central place for this. Click the three dots in the upper right corner of Chrome to open the menu, then click "Settings." In the settings page, look for "Privacy and security" on the left side and click on it. Find "Site settings" and click on that.

On the next page, you will see a list of permissions you can manage. Click on "Camera" to see all the websites that have camera permission. You will see each website listed with its current permission status. From this page, you can change permissions for any site, remove permissions entirely, or see which sites have asked for camera access in the past.

This is also a good place to review sites you may have forgotten about. Sometimes you grant camera permission temporarily and then never revoke it. Going through this list periodically helps you keep your permissions tidy.

## What Happens When You Block Camera Access

If you block camera access for a website, that site will not be able to use your camera at all. When you try to use a feature that requires the camera, you will typically see a message telling you that the camera is blocked. Some sites might work around this by showing a placeholder or letting you know you need to enable camera access, while others might not work at all without it.

Blocking camera access is the right move for websites where you do not need video functionality. It protects your privacy and prevents any accidental activation of your camera.

## What Happens When You Allow Camera Access

When you allow camera access for a website, that site can use your camera whenever you are on the site and the feature requires it. The website might use your camera for video calls, taking photos, recording videos, or other features. Just keep in mind that allowing access means the website can use the camera as long as you are browsing that site.

Some websites might show a small indicator in the Chrome tab when your camera is actively being used. This helps you know when the camera is on and sending video.

## Using the Ask Option

Setting a site to ask each time gives you the most control. Every time the website tries to use your camera, Chrome will pop up a small prompt asking if you want to allow it. You can choose to allow it just this once, always allow it, or block it. This is a good option for sites you use occasionally but do not want to give permanent access to.

However, if you find these prompts annoying, you might prefer to just set the permission to allow or block and be done with it.

## A Quick Way to Reset All Permissions

If you have made changes over time and want to start fresh, you can reset all site permissions in Chrome. Go to Settings, then Privacy and security, then Site settings. Look for a "Reset permissions" option at the bottom of the page. This will clear all custom permissions you have set, and every site will go back to asking you the first time it tries to access your camera.

This can be useful if you feel you have too many permissions set up or if something is not working as expected.

## Keeping Your Camera Secure

Always think twice before granting camera permission to a website. Only allow camera access to sites you trust and that genuinely need it for their service. If a website asks for camera access but you cannot see why it would need it, it is perfectly fine to block it or choose to ask each time.
