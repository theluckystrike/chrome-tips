# Chrome Extensions for Speed Reading

If you are looking for chrome extensions for speed reading, you probably feel like there are not enough hours in the day to get through all the articles, reports, and online content you need to consume. Whether you are a student researching for a paper, a professional staying up to date with industry news, or just someone who wants to read more books and articles, speed reading can transform how much you accomplish.

The reality is that most people read at an average speed of around 200 to 250 words per minute, but with practice and the right tools, you can significantly increase that number. Chrome extensions for speed reading are designed to help you process text faster, reduce subvocalization (saying words in your head), and eliminate distractions that slow you down.

## Why We Read Slower Than Necessary

There are several reasons why most people read slower than they could. First, websites are full of distractions. Ads, popups, navigation menus, and related article links all compete for your attention. Every time you get pulled away from the main text, you lose momentum and have to refocus.

Second, many websites have poor formatting. Small fonts, narrow text columns, cluttered layouts, and low contrast all make reading harder and slower. Your eyes have to work harder to track the text, which naturally slows you down.

Third, most people engage in subvocalization, which is silently pronouncing each word as they read it. This habits limits your reading speed to how fast you can speak, typically around 150 to 200 words per minute. Speed reading techniques and tools can help you reduce this mental habit and absorb information more quickly.

## How Speed Reading Extensions Work

Chrome extensions for speed reading tackle the problem in different ways. Some clean up web pages to remove distractions and present text in a more readable format. Others use techniques like rapid serial visual presentation, which shows words one at a time in a fixed position to help you read faster. Some extensions focus on formatting improvements, while others provide tools to train your reading skills.

The best chrome extensions for speed reading share a few characteristics. They are easy to use without complicated setup, they work on most websites you visit, and they let you customize settings to match your preferences. You should not need technical knowledge to install and use these tools effectively.

## Speed Reader by Microsoft

Speed Reader is an extension developed by Microsoft that uses proven speed reading techniques to help you consume content faster. It works by taking any article or web page and presenting the text in a clean, focused format. You can adjust the words per minute rate to find your comfortable speed, starting slower and gradually increasing as you get used to the format.

The extension uses something called RSVP, which stands for rapid serial visual presentation. Instead of your eyes having to scan across lines and down a page, each word appears briefly in the same position on your screen. This eliminates the time spent moving your eyes and helps you maintain focus on the content itself.

What makes Speed Reader particularly useful is its simplicity. You click the extension icon on any page, and it immediately transforms the content into a distraction-free reading mode. You can pause, rewind, and adjust speed as needed, which is helpful when you encounter complex passages that require more attention.

## Mercury Reader

Mercury Reader is another popular choice for those seeking chrome extensions for speed reading. While it is not specifically designed for speed reading, it creates optimal reading conditions that naturally help you read faster. The extension removes all clutter from web pages, including ads, sidebars, popups, and navigation elements, leaving only the main article text.

When you remove visual distractions, you can focus entirely on the content, which naturally increases your reading speed. Mercury Reader also lets you customize the appearance of the text. You can choose font styles, adjust text size, and select background colors that feel comfortable to you. Some readers find that a cream background reduces eye strain, while others prefer a clean white or dark mode.

The extension offers a keyboard shortcut, so you can activate it quickly without interrupting your workflow. Once you get used to using Mercury Reader, you will find that articles feel shorter and easier to process.

## Tab Suspender Pro

While Tab Suspender Pro is not specifically a speed reading extension, it plays an important role in helping you read faster. When you have dozens of tabs open in Chrome, your browser can become sluggish and slow. Pages take longer to load, scrolling becomes choppy, and your overall browsing experience suffers. This directly impacts your reading speed and comprehension.

Tab Suspender Pro automatically suspends tabs that you have not used recently, which frees up your computer's memory and processing power. This means the tab you are actively reading stays responsive and fast. The extension works quietly in the background, and when you click on a suspended tab, it reloads instantly.

By keeping your browser running smoothly, Tab Suspender Pro ensures that nothing interrupts your reading flow. A slow browser can be frustrating enough to make you abandon articles, but with this extension, you can maintain consistent reading speed without technical delays.

## Spreed

Spreed is an extension specifically designed for speed reading using the RSVP method. It takes text from web pages and displays it word by word at your chosen speed. You can start at a moderate pace and gradually increase the words per minute as you become more comfortable.

The extension allows you to read without the visual clutter of web pages. You see only the words you need to process, nothing else. This focused approach helps reduce distractions and trains your brain to read more efficiently. Spreed also remembers your preferred speed across sessions, so you do not have to adjust it every time you use it.

Many users find that RSVP-based reading takes some getting used to, but the results are worth the practice. Once you adapt to seeing words presented this way, you can read significantly faster than traditional methods.

## Readability

Readability is a simple but effective extension that makes web pages easier to read quickly. It strips away all non-essential content and presents the article in a clean, readable format. The extension offers several themes, including sepia, dark, and black modes, so you can choose what works best for your eyes.

When pages are easier to read, you naturally read faster. Readability removes the friction that comes with poor formatting and distracting elements. You can customize font size, line height, and margins to create your ideal reading environment.

The extension also lets you save articles to read later, which is helpful if you want to bookmark content for focused reading sessions. You can build a reading queue of articles and go through them efficiently when you have time.

## Tips for Getting the Most Out of Speed Reading Extensions

Using chrome extensions for speed reading is only part of the equation. To really boost your reading speed, practice a few habits alongside these tools. First, try to avoid subvocalization by focusing on the meaning of phrases rather than individual words. This takes practice, but speed reading extensions can help train your brain to absorb groups of words at once.

Second, set specific goals for your reading sessions. If you know you want to get through five articles, challenge yourself to maintain a steady pace. Having a clear target keeps you motivated and helps you push through when you would normally slow down or stop.

Third, take breaks. Speed reading can be mentally taxing, especially when you are first starting out. Give your brain time to process and consolidate what you have read. Short breaks between articles help maintain comprehension even as you increase speed.

Finally, remember that not every piece of content needs to be read at top speed. Technical articles, complex arguments, or material you need to remember well are worth slowing down for. Use speed reading extensions strategically for content that requires quick scanning, and take your time when it matters.

## Finding Your Perfect Speed Reading Setup

The best chrome extensions for speed reading are the ones that fit seamlessly into your browsing habits. Start with one extension and use it consistently for a few weeks. Pay attention to how it affects your reading speed and comprehension. If something does not feel right, try a different extension until you find the right fit.

Speed reading is a skill that improves with practice. The extensions mentioned here can help you read faster and more efficiently, but the real progress comes from regular use. Once you find your rhythm, you will be surprised at how much more content you can consume in the same amount of time.
