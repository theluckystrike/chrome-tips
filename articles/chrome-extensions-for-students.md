## Todoist: Master Your Academic Tasks

Managing multiple courses, assignments, deadlines, and extracurricular activities is one of the biggest challenges students face. Todoist is a powerful task management extension that can help you regain control over your academic responsibilities and ensure nothing falls through the cracks.

Todoist operates on a simple but powerful principle: capture everything you need to do in a organized system, break down large tasks into manageable steps, and review your commitments regularly to stay on track. The Chrome extension brings this functionality directly to your browser, allowing you to quickly add tasks, check deadlines, and organize your workload without switching between applications.

For students, the ability to organize tasks by project is particularly valuable. You can create separate projects for each of your courses, making it easy to see all assignments, readings, and deadlines related to a specific class. Within each project, you can break down larger assignments into smaller subtasks. For example, a research paper project might include subtasks like choosing a topic, conducting preliminary research, creating an outline, writing the first draft, and proofreading. This decomposition makes overwhelming assignments feel much more manageable and helps you make consistent progress.

The extension also offers convenient features for recurring tasks. Many academic responsibilities repeat on a schedule—weekly reading assignments, bi-weekly problem sets, monthly progress reports. Todoist allows you to set up recurring tasks so that these regular commitments are automatically added to your task list at the appropriate intervals. This automation saves you from having to manually re-enter recurring tasks and ensures you never forget an upcoming deadline.

One of Todoist's most useful features for students is its integration with the browser. You can quickly add any webpage as a task, which is perfect for saving articles to read later, marking course websites to revisit, or creating links to important resources. When you add a task from a webpage, Todoist automatically captures the URL, making it easy to find the related content when you are ready to work on that task.

The collaboration features in Todoist also make it easy to work on group projects. You can share projects with classmates, assign tasks to different group members, and track progress together. This transparency helps ensure everyone knows what they are responsible for and can see how the project is progressing. For students who struggle with group coordination, Todoist provides a structured framework that can make collaborative work much smoother.

The Chrome extension includes a badge that shows your task count for today, giving you a constant gentle reminder of what you need to accomplish. This visibility can help you stay focused and resist the temptation to procrastinate. When you complete a task, the satisfaction of checking it off provides a small but meaningful reward that can help maintain motivation throughout long study sessions.

Whether you are studying in a noisy dorm room, a bustling coffee shop, or just struggling to concentrate in a quiet apartment, the right background sounds can make a remarkable difference in your ability to focus and retain information. Noisli is a Chrome extension that provides precisely this kind of environmental control.

Noisli allows you to mix different ambient sounds to create a personalized soundscape that suits your study needs. The extension includes a variety of sounds such as rain, thunder, wind, forest ambiance, white noise, pink noise, coffee shop chatter, and fireplace crackles. You can adjust the volume of each sound individually and save your favorite combinations for quick access later.

The science behind Noisli's effectiveness lies in its ability to mask distracting noises. When you are trying to study in a noisy environment, unpredictable sounds can constantly break your concentration. By playing consistent background noise, you create a auditory buffer that makes those sudden distractions less noticeable. Different sounds work better for different people and different types of work—some students find that white noise helps them focus, while others prefer the more natural sounds of rain or a forest.

One of Noisli's most valuable features is its timer functionality. You can set study sessions of various lengths, and the extension will play your selected sounds for that duration. This structured approach to study time can help combat procrastination by giving you a clear endpoint for your study session. Many students find that knowing they only need to focus for a set period makes it easier to get started, and once they are in the flow of studying, they often find they want to continue beyond the timer.

The extension also includes a text editor with a distraction-free interface that you can use while listening to your soundscapes. This feature is perfect for those times when you want to do some writing or note-taking without the visual clutter of a full-featured word processor. Combined with your custom soundscape, this minimal writing environment can help you enter a state of deep focus that promotes productivity.

For students who study in different locations throughout the day, Noisli's preset soundscapes can be a real asset. You might find that rain sounds work best for morning study sessions in your dorm room, while coffee shop ambiance helps you concentrate when you are working from a campus café. Having these presets ready to go means you can quickly optimize your environment whenever you sit down to study.

Many students also find that Noisli helps with relaxation and sleep. The same sounds that help you focus during study sessions can also help you wind down after a long day of classes. Some sounds, like rain or thunder, are particularly effective for creating a peaceful atmosphere that promotes restful sleep. This versatility makes Noisli a valuable tool for your entire student life, not just for study sessions.

## Tab Suspender Pro: Keep Your Browser Running Fast

Modern students often have dozens of tabs open at once—course materials, research articles, assignment descriptions, communication tools, and reference resources. All these tabs consume memory and processing power, which can slow your browser to a crawl, especially on older computers or those with limited RAM. Tab Suspender Pro addresses this problem by automatically suspending tabs that you are not actively using, freeing up resources while keeping your place so you can return to them easily.

When Tab Suspender Pro suspends a tab, it replaces the page content with a lightweight placeholder that shows the page title and favicon. The tab remains in your browser window exactly where you left it, so your organization remains intact. When you click on a suspended tab to return to it, the extension quickly reloads the page so you can continue exactly where you left off. This seamless experience means you never lose your place, even as you accumulate dozens of open tabs throughout your study session.

For students who keep many research sources open while working on papers, Tab Suspender Pro can dramatically improve browser performance. Those PDF articles, academic databases, and reference websites that you are not actively reading are consuming memory in the background. Once suspended, they stop using system resources until you need them again. This optimization can make the difference between a browser that runs smoothly and one that freezes or crashes during important study sessions.

The extension includes smart features that allow you to customize its behavior. You can whitelist websites that should never be suspended, ensuring that critical tools like your learning management system or communication platforms remain active. You can also set custom suspension delays if you want tabs to remain active for longer before being suspended. These options allow you to balance resource savings with convenience based on your specific workflow.

For students working on research-heavy projects with many open sources, combining Tab Suspender Pro with Zotero creates a particularly powerful workflow. You can save sources to your Zotero library for long-term organization while using Tab Suspender Pro to keep your browser responsive. This approach lets you maintain access to dozens of relevant sources without sacrificing performance, making it easier to switch between materials as you write and research.
