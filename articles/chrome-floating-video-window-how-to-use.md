# Chrome Floating Video Window How to Use

If you want to watch a video while browsing other websites in Chrome, a floating video window is exactly what you need. Chrome offers a built-in feature called Picture in Picture that lets you pull any video out of its webpage and keep it floating above everything else on your screen. This way, you can work, browse, or chat while your video plays in a small corner window.

## What Is a Floating Video Window

A floating video window is a small, movable window that stays on top of your other applications and browser tabs. Instead of keeping a video stuck inside one webpage, you can detach it and place it anywhere on your screen. The video continues playing even when you switch to a different tab or application entirely.

This feature is incredibly useful for people who like to multitask. You might want to watch a tutorial while taking notes, follow a live stream while working on a document, or keep a news broadcast playing while browsing social media. The floating window stays visible regardless of what else you are doing on your computer.

Chrome's Picture in Picture mode is the built-in way to create these floating video windows. It works with most video websites and gives you full control over the window size and position.

## How to Activate Floating Video Window

Using Chrome floating video windows is straightforward. Here are the main ways to activate this feature.

The quickest method is to look for the Picture in Picture button directly on the video player. When you hover your mouse over a video on websites like YouTube, Vimeo, or other video platforms, you will usually see a small icon that looks like a rectangle with a smaller rectangle inside it. Click this button and the video will instantly pop out into a floating window.

If you cannot find the button, try right-clicking directly on the video itself. A context menu will appear, and near the bottom you should see an option that says "Picture in Picture." Click on it and the video will open in a floating window.

Some videos on certain websites do not show the Picture in Picture button. In those cases, the right-click method usually still works, though there are some exceptions with premium streaming services.

## Moving and Resizing the Floating Window

Once your video is in floating mode, you have complete control over its placement and size.

To move the window, simply click and drag anywhere on the floating window itself. You can place it in any corner of your screen or position it anywhere that is convenient for you. Most people prefer the bottom-right corner since it stays out of the way while still being visible.

To resize the window, hover your mouse over any edge or corner of the floating window until your cursor changes to a resize indicator. Then click and drag to make the window larger or smaller. This is helpful when you need to see more detail in the video or when you want to keep it particularly small and unobtrusive.

The floating window will remember its position even when you switch between tabs, so you do not need to reposition it every time.

## Common Uses for Floating Video Windows

There are many situations where Chrome floating video windows come in handy.

Following along with tutorials is one of the most popular uses. If you are learning a new skill through a video tutorial, you can keep the instructions visible in a small window while practicing in another application or website.

Watching live streams or webinars while working is another common scenario. You can keep a conference, gaming stream, or podcast playing in the corner without missing out on your actual work.

Fitness enthusiasts often use this feature to follow workout videos. Position the floating window near your exercise space and follow along without constantly looking back at your computer screen.

Some people simply enjoy having music videos or ambient content playing while they browse. The audio continues even if you minimize the window or cover it with other applications.

## Tips for Getting the Most Out of Floating Windows

Here are some helpful suggestions to make your experience with Chrome floating video windows better.

Keep the window small when you only need audio. If you are just listening to music or a podcast, shrinking the window to its minimum size keeps it even less distracting while still letting you control playback.

Use keyboard shortcuts to manage your floating window more efficiently. Pressing the Escape key while the floating window is active will return the video to its original webpage.

Remember that the floating window operates independently from the original tab. Even if you close the original tab, the floating window will continue playing until you close it or until the video ends.

If you find yourself using floating windows frequently, be aware that multiple videos playing simultaneously can use more system resources. Consider closing floating windows when you are done watching to keep your browser running smoothly.

## Troubleshooting Floating Video Windows

Sometimes Chrome floating video windows do not work as expected. Here are solutions to common problems.

The Picture in Picture button is missing from a video. Some websites intentionally disable this feature, particularly streaming services like Netflix, Hulu, or Disney+ due to content licensing restrictions. Try using the right-click method instead, though it may also be disabled on these platforms.

The video keeps pausing when you switch tabs. Make sure the original tab with the video remains loaded in your browser, even if it is not the active tab. Chrome should continue playing the video in the floating window, but the original tab needs to stay open.

The floating window disappears unexpectedly. If this happens, go back to the original video and activate Picture in Picture again. Sometimes browser updates or website changes can temporarily affect the feature.

Audio is not playing. Check that your system volume is turned up and that the floating window is not muted. There is a small volume control directly on the floating window that you can adjust.

## When Floating Windows Are Not Available

There are certain situations where Chrome floating video windows will not work.

Premium streaming platforms often block Picture in Picture to enforce their viewing policies. Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+, and similar services commonly disable this feature on their content.

Some websites built with certain technologies may not support floating video windows due to how their video players are designed.

Corporate or school networks sometimes block Picture in Picture through their content filtering systems for productivity or security reasons.

In these situations, you will need to watch videos in regular tabs rather than in floating windows.

## Give It a Try

Chrome floating video windows are simple to use and can significantly improve your browsing experience. The next time you need to watch something while working or browsing, try pulling it out into a floating window. It only takes a moment to activate and can make multitasking much easier.
