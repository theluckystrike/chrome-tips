Chrome follow website feature what it does is a question that comes up for many users who want to keep track of their favorite websites without missing new content. If you have sites you visit regularly, whether they are blogs, news outlets, or any website that publishes fresh content, understanding how Chrome helps you follow these sites can make your browsing much more organized.

## What the Follow Feature Actually Does

When you use Chrome to follow a website, you are essentially setting up a way to remember that you want to see updates from that site. Chrome does not have a single built-in button called "Follow" that works like social media, but the browser offers several practical methods to help you stay updated on the content you care about.

The main idea behind following websites in Chrome is that you want to know when something new appears without having to remember to check each site manually. Chrome provides different approaches to this, and each one works a bit differently depending on your needs.

## How Bookmarks Work for Following Sites

The simplest way to keep track of a website is through bookmarks. When you find a site you want to follow, click the star icon in the address bar or press Ctrl+D on Windows or Cmd+D on Mac to bookmark it. Give the bookmark a name you will recognize later, and consider creating a folder specifically for sites you want to follow.

This method works well because your bookmarks sync automatically when you sign into Chrome with your Google account. You can access your followed sites from any device where you use Chrome. The limitation is that bookmarks do not notify you when new content appears. You have to remember to open your bookmarks and visit each site to see if there is something new.

## Using Reading List for Content Tracking

Chrome includes a Reading List feature that serves a slightly different purpose. When you come across an article you want to read later, you can save it by clicking the Bookmarks icon in the address bar and selecting "Add to Reading List." This is different from regular bookmarks because it is designed specifically for individual articles rather than entire websites.

While the Reading List does not notify you about new content from a website, it does sync across all your devices. This means you can save interesting articles to read later and they will be available whether you are on your computer, tablet, or phone. For following websites, you would still need to remember which sites you want to track and visit them manually.

## Following Through RSS Feeds

RSS feeds provide a more powerful way to follow websites, though they require some additional setup. Many websites offer RSS feeds that list their latest content in a standardized format. You can usually find these by adding "/feed" or "/rss" to the website URL, though not all sites offer this feature.

To use RSS feeds in Chrome, you need to install an RSS reader extension from the Chrome Web Store. Once installed, these extensions allow you to subscribe to websites and see their latest posts in a single feed. The extension will guide you through adding sites to your subscription list, and you can then check one place to see what is new from all the sites you follow.

This method gives you the closest experience to traditional following because you get a feed of all new content without visiting each website individually. The trade-off is that you need to set up the extension and learn how to use it, but many people find the convenience worth the initial effort.

## Website Notifications System

Some websites have their own notification systems built in. When you visit a site and see a prompt asking to allow notifications, you can choose to allow it if you want to receive alerts about new content. The website will then send notifications through Chrome whenever they publish something new.

To manage which sites can notify you, look for the bell or speaker icon in the address bar when you are on a site that has notification permission. From there, you can see the full list of sites that can send you notifications and change those permissions as needed.

It is worth being selective about which sites you allow to send notifications. If you allow too many sites, you might end up with constant interruptions that defeat the purpose of trying to organize your content consumption.

## Third-Party Tools That Can Help

If you find that Chrome's built-in options are not quite meeting your needs for following websites, there are third-party tools designed specifically for this purpose. These services act as intermediaries between you and the websites you want to follow, collecting updates and presenting them in an organized way.

Some of these tools work as browser extensions while others are web-based services. They typically allow you to add websites to a watch list, and the tool will notify you when new content appears. This can be especially useful if you want to follow many sites and need a central place to see all the updates.

Tab Suspender Pro is one such tool that can help you manage your browser experience while staying organized. While it is primarily designed to automatically pause tabs you are not using to save memory and improve performance, it also helps you keep track of which sites you have open and which ones you want to revisit for new content. This can complement your follow strategy by ensuring your browser runs efficiently while you keep track of the sites that matter to you.

## Choosing the Right Method for You

The best approach to following websites in Chrome depends on how you like to consume content. If you only follow a few sites, simple bookmarks might be enough and require no extra setup. If you follow many websites or want a centralized feed where you can see everything at once, RSS readers are worth the initial setup time. If you want to be notified immediately when new content appears, allowing website notifications or using a third-party service makes more sense.

Think about how you actually browse and what will fit into your routine. The most sophisticated system will not help if you forget to use it. Choose the method that matches your habits and sticking with it will be much easier.
