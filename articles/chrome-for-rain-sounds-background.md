In a completely quiet room, even minor interruptions like a door closing, someone talking in another room, or notification sounds can break your concentration. Rain sounds create what is called a sound mask, covering up these sudden distractions and helping your brain maintain focus on the task at hand.

Many people also find that rain sounds help them feel less isolated while working alone. The gentle patter of rain creates a sense of company without the loneliness of complete silence, and it can make long work sessions feel more comfortable and enjoyable.
