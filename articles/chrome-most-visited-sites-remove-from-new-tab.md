# How to Remove Most Visited Sites from Chrome New Tab Page

When you open a new tab in Google Chrome, you are greeted by a collection of thumbnail tiles showing your most frequently visited websites. This feature, called the Most Visited sites, is designed to help you reach your favorite destinations with a single click. However, many users find these thumbnails intrusive, unnecessary, or simply want a cleaner browsing experience. If you are looking for ways to remove most visited sites from your Chrome new tab page, this guide will walk you through several effective methods.

## Understanding Chrome Most Visited Sites

Chrome automatically tracks your browsing activity to generate these quick-access tiles. The algorithm considers factors like how often you visit a site and how recently you opened it. While this can be convenient for power users who frequently navigate between the same handful of websites, it can also feel like an invasion of privacy for those who prefer more control over their browser interface.

The good news is that Chrome provides several ways to manage or remove these suggested sites. You can either clear your browsing data temporarily or disable the feature entirely through Chrome settings or extensions.

## Method One: Clear Browsing History

The simplest way to remove most visited sites from Chrome is to clear your browsing history. This approach removes all thumbnails and replaces them with either empty tiles or fresh suggestions based on your remaining activity.

To clear your browsing history in Chrome, follow these steps:

1. Click the three-dot menu icon in the top-right corner of your browser window
2. Select "History" and then click "History" again from the dropdown menu
3. Click "Clear browsing data" on the left sidebar
4. Choose the time range for which you want to delete data. Selecting "All time" will remove all thumbnails completely
5. Make sure "Browsing history" is checked, then click "Clear data"

After clearing your history, open a new tab. You will notice that the Most Visited section now shows fewer tiles or appears empty. Over time, as you continue browsing, new sites will gradually reappear based on your activity. If you want to prevent them from coming back, you will need to explore other methods.

## Method Two: Disable the Most Visited Feature in Chrome Settings

Chrome allows you to turn off the Most Visited sites feature entirely through its settings. This is a more permanent solution if you do not want to see these thumbnails at all.

To disable Most Visited sites in Chrome settings, follow these steps:

1. Type `chrome://settings/appearance` in the address bar and press Enter
2. Look for an option called "Show shortcuts" or "Most visited sites" 
3. Toggle the switch to the off position

The exact wording may vary depending on your Chrome version, but you should find an option related to showing shortcuts or visited sites on the new tab page. Once disabled, your new tab page will display only the search box and any custom background images you have set, without any website thumbnails.

## Method Three: Use Chrome Flags for Additional Control

Chrome offers experimental features through its flags page that provide even more control over the new tab page. This method is slightly more technical but offers granular customization options.

To access Chrome flags:

1. Type `chrome://flags` in the address bar and press Enter
2. Search for "Top Chrome" or "New Tab" related experiments
3. Look for options like "OneGoogleBar" or "Most Visited" and adjust their settings

Be cautious when modifying flags, as experimental features can sometimes cause unexpected behavior. If you encounter any issues, you can always reset all flags to their default state by clicking the "Reset all" button at the top of the flags page.

## Method Four: Use an Extension for Complete Control

For users who want maximum control over their new tab page, browser extensions offer the most flexible solution. There are several Chrome extensions available that replace the default new tab page with a custom interface, giving you full control over what appears and how it looks.

One popular option is Tab Suspender Pro, which not only removes the Most Visited sites but also helps improve browser performance by suspending inactive tabs. This extension gives you a clean, distraction-free new tab experience while adding useful productivity features. Many users find that extensions like this provide the best balance between functionality and customization.

To install an extension:

1. Open the Chrome Web Store
2. Search for "new tab page replacement" or a specific extension like Tab Suspender Pro
3. Click "Add to Chrome" and confirm the permissions
4. Configure the extension settings according to your preferences

Extensions can also help if you want to keep certain shortcuts visible while hiding others, giving you a hybrid approach that combines convenience with privacy.

## Maintaining Your Privacy

Beyond removing Most Visited sites from your new tab page, you might want to consider other privacy measures. Chrome stores various types of data that can reveal your browsing habits, including cookies, cached images, and download history. Regularly clearing this data or using Chrome's enhanced protection settings can help keep your browsing private.

If you use Chrome across multiple devices and are signed in with your Google account, your browsing data may sync between devices. To prevent this, go to Chrome sync settings and adjust what data gets synced or use a separate profile for private browsing.

## Conclusion

Removing Most Visited sites from your Chrome new tab page is straightforward once you know which method works best for your needs. Whether you prefer the quick fix of clearing your history, the permanence of disabling the feature in settings, or the flexibility of using an extension like Tab Suspender Pro, you can achieve a cleaner, more private browsing experience.

Take some time to explore these options and choose the approach that fits your workflow. A customized new tab page can significantly improve your daily browsing comfort and help you stay focused on what matters most.
