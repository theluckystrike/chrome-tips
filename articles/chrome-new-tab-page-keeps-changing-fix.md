# Chrome New Tab Page Keeps Changing Fix

Chrome new tab page keeps changing is a frustrating problem that many Chrome users encounter. You set up your preferred homepage or new tab page exactly how you like it, only to find that it has been replaced by something else the next time you open Chrome. This can happen because of extensions, software changes on your computer, or settings that got modified without you realizing it. The good news is that you can fix this issue and take back control of your browsing experience.

Let me walk you through why this happens and what you can do to stop it.

## Why Your Chrome New Tab Page Keeps Changing

There are several common reasons why your new tab page might keep changing. Understanding these causes will help you identify which one is affecting your browser.

One of the most common culprits is browser extensions. Some extensions are designed to change your new tab page as part of their functionality. Weather extensions, note-taking tools, and even some ad blockers may redirect your new tab to show their own content or display search results. Sometimes you intentionally install these extensions, but there are also cases where malicious or poorly designed extensions modify your settings without clear permission.

Another reason could be unwanted software or browser hijackers. These are programs that install themselves on your computer and change your browser settings, including your homepage and new tab page. They often redirect you to specific websites that the developers want you to visit, which could be search engines, shopping sites, or other pages that generate revenue for them.

Your Chrome settings might also have been changed accidentally. It is easy to click through setup wizards or software installations without paying close attention to what you are agreeing to. Some free software packages include offers to change your browser settings, and if you do not deselect those options, they will modify your Chrome configuration.

Finally, if you use multiple devices and sync your Chrome data, changes made on one device can propagate to others. If your settings were changed on your phone or another computer, those changes could appear on your desktop Chrome through sync.

## How to Fix Your Chrome New Tab Page

Now that you understand why this happens, let us look at practical steps you can take to fix it.

### Check Your Chrome Settings

The first step is to verify and adjust your Chrome settings directly. Open Chrome and click the three dots in the upper right corner to access the menu. Select Settings, then look for the On startup section on the left sidebar. Click on it and check what option is selected.

If you want your new tab page to remain blank or display your preferred homepage, choose the option that says Open a new tab or enter a specific URL. Make sure the URL listed is exactly what you want. If you see an unfamiliar website listed, remove it and add your preferred URL instead.

Also check the Search engine section in your settings. Sometimes browser hijackers will change your default search engine as well. Ensure your preferred search engine is selected and remove any unfamiliar ones from the list of options.

### Remove Problematic Extensions

Extensions are often the reason why your new tab page keeps changing. To check which extensions you have installed, type chrome://extensions in your address bar and press Enter. This will show you all your installed extensions.

Look through the list and identify any extensions that you do not remember installing, that you no longer use, or that seem suspicious. Remove these extensions by clicking the Remove button. Pay special attention to extensions that claim to offer free features, weather updates, coupons, or productivity tools, as these are common carriers for unwanted behavior.

If you find that a specific extension is causing the problem but you still want to use it, check its settings to see if you can disable the new tab modification. Some extensions allow you to turn off this feature while keeping the rest of the functionality.

### Reset Chrome to Default Settings

If the above steps do not resolve the issue, you can reset Chrome to its default settings. This will restore all settings to their original state and remove most extensions and customizations. Note that this will also clear your saved passwords and other data, so make sure you have that information backed up.

To reset Chrome, go to Settings, then click on Reset and cleanup on the left sidebar. Select Restore settings to their original defaults and confirm the reset. After Chrome restarts, you can reinstall your extensions one by one to identify which one might be causing the problem.

### Use a Dedicated Extension to Control Your New Tab

If you want a more permanent solution and more control over what appears on your new tab page, consider using a dedicated extension designed for this purpose. Tab Suspender Pro is one option that allows you to customize your new tab page while also helping you manage tab memory usage. It gives you the ability to set your preferred URL, choose a blank page, or use a custom design. This type of extension puts you in control of what you see every time you open a new tab.

### Scan for Malware

If you suspect that unwanted software might be installed on your computer, it is worth running a malware scan. Use your antivirus software to perform a full system scan and remove any threats that are detected. You can also use built-in tools like Chrome's cleanup tool, which you can access by typing chrome://settings/cleanup in your address bar. This tool scans for and removes software that may be causing problems with Chrome.

## Preventing Future Changes

After fixing the issue, there are some steps you can take to prevent it from happening again. Be careful about what software you install on your computer and always read the fine print during installation. Look for checkboxes that mention changing your browser settings and make sure they are unchecked if you do not want those changes.

Only install extensions from trusted sources and take a moment to read reviews and check permissions before adding anything to your browser. If an extension asks for more permissions than it seems to need, that is a red flag.

Keep your Chrome browser updated to the latest version, as updates often include security improvements that protect against unwanted modifications.

## Conclusion

Having your Chrome new tab page keeps changing can be annoying, but it is a problem you can solve. By checking your settings, removing problematic extensions, resetting Chrome if needed, and being mindful about what you install, you can regain control of your browsing experience. Whether you prefer a simple blank page, your favorite website, or a custom new tab setup, you deserve to have Chrome behave the way you want it to.
