# Chrome Noise Cancellation for Meetings

Nothing disrupts a productive video meeting quite like unexpected background noise. Whether it is a barking dog, a loud neighbor, or the hum of your air conditioner, these sounds can distract participants and make communication difficult. Fortunately, Chrome offers several ways to reduce or eliminate background noise during video calls. In this guide, we will explore the built-in noise cancellation features available in Chrome and Google Meet, as well as third-party extensions that can further improve your audio quality.

## Why Noise Cancellation Matters for Video Meetings

Clear audio is essential for effective communication in virtual meetings. When background noise is present, participants must frequently ask others to repeat themselves, which wastes time and reduces meeting productivity. In professional settings, excessive noise can make you appear unprepared or unprofessional. For remote workers and anyone who attends multiple video calls daily, having reliable noise cancellation is no longer a luxury but a necessity.

Chrome-based browsers have made significant strides in noise cancellation technology. Whether you use Google Meet, Zoom through the browser, Microsoft Teams, or other video conferencing platforms, you can leverage Chrome's audio processing capabilities to deliver cleaner sound.

## Built-in Noise Cancellation in Google Meet

If you use Google Meet for your video calls, you will be pleased to know that Google has integrated noise cancellation directly into the service. This feature uses machine learning to identify and filter out background sounds while preserving your voice.

### How to Enable Noise Cancellation in Google Meet

**For scheduled meetings:**

1. Open Google Meet and start or join a meeting
2. Click on the three dots (more options) in the bottom right corner
3. Select "Settings" from the menu
4. Go to the "Audio" tab
5. Toggle "Noise cancellation" to on

**For instant meetings:**

1. Click on the "New Meeting" button in Google Meet
2. Select "Start an instant meeting"
3. Once in the meeting, click on the three dots in the bottom right
4. Access Settings and enable noise cancellation under the Audio tab

Google Meet offers two levels of noise cancellation: "Noise cancellation" and "Enhanced noise cancellation." The enhanced option provides stronger filtering for particularly noisy environments, though it may require more processing power from your computer.

It is worth noting that noise cancellation in Google Meet works best when you have a decent microphone. If you are using a low-quality built-in microphone, the feature may not perform as effectively. Consider upgrading to a dedicated microphone or a quality headset for optimal results.

## Chrome Flags for Audio Processing

Chrome includes experimental features that can improve audio quality across various web applications. While these flags are not specifically designed for noise cancellation in meetings, they can enhance overall audio processing.

To access Chrome flags:

1. Type `chrome://flags` in your address bar
2. Press Enter to view the experimental features
3. Search for "audio" to see relevant options

Look for features like "Audio API" improvements or "WebRTC" enhancements. These experimental settings may provide marginal improvements to audio quality, though they are not a substitute for dedicated noise cancellation.

For the most stable experience, stick with the noise cancellation features built into your video conferencing platform rather than relying on Chrome flags.

## Third-Party Extensions for Noise Cancellation

While Google Meet has built-in noise cancellation, other video platforms may not offer this feature. Fortunately, several Chrome extensions can add noise cancellation capabilities to any website that uses your microphone.

### Krisp

Krisp is one of the most popular noise cancellation extensions available. It works by processing your audio through Krisp's servers to remove background noise before transmitting it to the meeting application. The free version includes 60 minutes of noise-free calling per week, which may be sufficient for casual users.

To use Krisp:

1. Visit the Chrome Web Store and search for "Krisp"
2. Click "Add to Chrome" to install the extension
3. Create a Krisp account or sign in
4. Grant the necessary microphone permissions
5. During your meeting, click the Krisp icon in your toolbar to enable noise cancellation

Krisp also offers a desktop application that can provide additional features and unlimited noise cancellation for those who need it.

### Soli

Soli is another excellent extension for removing background noise. It uses advanced audio processing to filter out unwanted sounds in real-time. The extension is free to use and does not require creating an account.

### Noise Blocker

For those who want to block specific types of noise rather than all background sounds, Noise Blocker allows you to define which sounds to suppress. You can train the extension to recognize particular noises in your environment and automatically mute them during calls.

## Optimizing Your Chrome Settings for Better Meeting Audio

Beyond enabling noise cancellation, there are several Chrome settings you can adjust to improve your overall meeting experience.

### Enable WebRTC Noise Gain

WebRTC is the technology that powers real-time communication in Chrome. Enabling noise gain can help maintain consistent audio levels:

1. Go to `chrome://flags`
2. Search for "WebRTC"
3. Look for "WebRTC Audio Gain Control" and enable it

### Manage Tab Suspenders During Meetings

If you use extensions like Tab Suspender Pro to manage browser memory, ensure that your video conferencing tab is excluded from suspension. Some tab suspenders may accidentally pause or reload your meeting tab during periods of inactivity, which can disrupt your call. Add your frequently used meeting platforms to the extension's whitelist to prevent this issue.

### Keep Chrome Updated

Google regularly updates Chrome with improvements to audio processing and WebRTC functionality. Ensure your browser is always up to date by checking for updates in the Chrome menu under "Help" and "About Google Chrome."

## Tips for Crystal-Clear Meeting Audio

Even with noise cancellation enabled, following these best practices will help ensure your audio comes through clearly:

**Use a quality microphone:** A built-in laptop microphone may pick up more ambient noise than a dedicated headset or external microphone. Investing in a decent headset with a noise-canceling microphone can significantly improve audio quality.

**Minimize background noise before meetings:** Close windows facing noisy streets, turn off fans or air conditioners if possible, and inform household members about your meeting schedule.

**Use headphones:** Wearing headphones prevents your microphone from picking up audio from your speakers, which can create echo and feedback issues.

**Speak clearly and at a consistent volume:** Machine learning algorithms work best when your voice is clear and consistent. Avoid shouting or speaking too quietly.

**Test your audio before important meetings:** Most video platforms offer a way to test your microphone. Use this feature to ensure everything is working correctly before joining an important call.

## Conclusion

Chrome noise cancellation for meetings has never been more accessible. Whether you rely on Google Meet's built-in features or use third-party extensions like Krisp, eliminating background noise is achievable with minimal effort. By enabling the appropriate settings and following best practices, you can ensure that your voice comes through clearly in every meeting, helping you communicate more effectively and project professionalism.

Take a few minutes to configure your noise cancellation settings today. Your colleagues will notice the difference, and you will wonder how you ever managed without it.
