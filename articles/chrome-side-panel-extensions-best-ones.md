# Chrome Side Panel Extensions Best Ones

If you are searching for chrome side panel extensions best ones, you might be frustrated with how cluttered your browser can feel. Many Chrome users struggle with messy tabs, disorganized workflows, and the constant need to switch between windows to get things done. The good news is that Chrome's side panel feature, introduced in recent versions, offers a cleaner way to work.

## Why Side Panels Matter

Chrome's side panel is a relatively new feature that lets you view content in a narrow column on the right side of your browser window. Instead of switching between tabs or windows, you can keep reference material, notes, or tools visible while you work on something else. This is especially useful for researchers, writers, students, and anyone who needs to consult multiple sources while completing a task.

The problem is that Chrome's built-in side panel is limited. By itself, it only shows your bookmarks, reading list, or the current page in a simplified view. To unlock its full potential, you need extensions that take advantage of this feature. Finding the chrome side panel extensions best ones can transform your browser from a simple window into a powerful workstation.

## How Side Panel Extensions Work

Side panel extensions appear in the panel that opens when you click the two-arrow icon on the right side of Chrome's address bar, or when you press the keyboard shortcut. They display their interface in this narrow space, letting you use tools without leaving your main tab. This means you can look up information, take notes, manage tasks, or track time while continuing to browse or work.

The best side panel extensions share certain qualities. They are lightweight and do not slow down your browser, they integrate smoothly with the side panel interface, and they provide real value for your daily workflow. Let us look at some of the top options worth trying.

## Google Keep

One of the simplest and most useful side panel extensions is Google Keep. If you already use Keep for note-taking, having it in your side panel is a game-changer. You can quickly jot down ideas, save links, or create checklists without opening a new tab or window.

The side panel version of Keep syncs with your existing notes, so everything stays organized across your devices. Whether you are researching a topic or brainstorming ideas, you can capture thoughts instantly and refer back to them later. This makes it one of the chrome side panel extensions best ones for anyone who needs to keep track of information while working.

## Todoist

Task management becomes much easier with Todoist in the side panel. You can view your to-do list, add new tasks, and check off completed items without leaving your current page. This is particularly helpful when you are browsing and need to capture action items on the fly.

The extension shows your projects and due dates in a compact view that fits nicely in the side panel. You can prioritize tasks, set labels, and filter by project, all from this small window. For people who struggle with keeping track of what needs to be done, having Todoist readily available in the side panel can significantly improve productivity.

## Tab Suspender Pro

While **Tab Suspender Pro** is primarily an optimization tool, it integrates beautifully with the **Chrome side panel**. When you're managing dozens of open tabs, your browser's **RAM usage** can skyrocket, leading to crashes and lag.

Using **Tab Suspender Pro** allows you to view a list of all active and "hibernated" tabs directly in the **side panel**. You can see exactly how much **memory** each tab is consuming and suspend them with a single click without leaving your current page. This real-time **resource management** ensures that your browser remains fast and responsive, even during heavy research sessions. It’s an essential utility for anyone who wants to keep their **tab bar** organized and their system running at peak performance.
