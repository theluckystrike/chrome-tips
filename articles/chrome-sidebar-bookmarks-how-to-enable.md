# Chrome Sidebar Bookmarks How to Enable

You have been searching for "chrome sidebar bookmarks how to enable" because you want a quicker way to access your saved websites without clicking through multiple menus. Let me walk you through the different ways you can get a bookmarks sidebar working in Chrome.

Chrome does not have a built-in bookmarks sidebar feature like some other browsers do, but you have several practical options to achieve that same convenient access to your bookmarks.

## Using Chrome is Sidebar Feature

Chrome introduced a side panel feature that you might find useful. This is not specifically a bookmarks sidebar, but it does offer quick access to some of your browser information.

To open the side panel in Chrome, look for the icon on the right side of your address bar. It typically looks like a square with lines inside. Click on it and you will see options for your browsing history, saved passwords, and other items. This is not exactly a bookmarks sidebar, but it does provide a convenient panel that sits alongside your web content.

If you want to access your bookmarks through this panel, click the puzzle piece icon within the side panel to see your extensions. Some bookmark extensions will appear here, giving you another way to reach your saved sites.

## Installing a Bookmark Sidebar Extension

The most common way to get a true bookmarks sidebar in Chrome is by using an extension from the Chrome Web Store. There are several options available, and most of them are free to use.

One popular choice is the Bookmark Manager extension, which provides a clean sidebar interface for browsing through your saved bookmarks. You can organize them into folders, search quickly through your collection, and open bookmarks with a single click.

Another option worth considering is the Sidebar Bookmarks extension. This places a collapsible sidebar on the left side of your browser window that shows your bookmark folders and sites. You can toggle it open or closed with a keyboard shortcut or by clicking the extension icon.

For users who want more advanced features, some extensions offer additional functionality like bookmark synchronization, cloud backup, and customizable appearance options. Tab Suspender Pro is another extension that works alongside your bookmarks setup, helping you manage open tabs while keeping your bookmark organization separate.

When choosing an extension, take a moment to check the permissions it requests. Stick with well-reviewed extensions that have been available for a while, as these tend to be more reliable and trustworthy.

## Setting Up Your Sidebar Extension

Once you have installed a bookmark sidebar extension, you will want to configure it to work the way you prefer.

Most sidebar extensions add an icon to your Chrome toolbar. Click this icon to open or close the sidebar. You can usually drag the icon to reposition it if you want it in a more convenient spot.

The first time you open your new bookmarks sidebar, take a moment to organize your bookmarks if needed. Create folders for different categories of sites, move your most frequently visited bookmarks to the top, and delete any bookmarks you no longer need. This preparation makes your sidebar much more useful day to day.

Many extensions let you customize the sidebar appearance. You can usually adjust the width, choose different themes or colors, and set whether the sidebar should remember its open or closed state between browsing sessions.

## Using Bookmarks Without a Full Sidebar

If you find that a full sidebar takes up too much screen space, there are lighter alternatives worth considering.

Chrome's bookmark manager itself is quite capable. You can access it by typing chrome://bookmarks in your address bar or by using the keyboard shortcut Ctrl+Shift+O on Windows or Cmd+Shift+O on Mac. This opens a new tab with all your bookmarks organized in a tree structure. You can search through your bookmarks, create new folders, and manage your collection from this central location.

Another approach is to pin your most important bookmarks to the Chrome bookmark bar, which sits right below the address bar. While this is not a sidebar, it gives you instant access to your top sites with a single click. You can have both a bookmark bar and a sidebar extension if you want maximum flexibility.

## Keeping Your Bookmarks Accessible Across Devices

If you use Chrome sync, your bookmarks automatically appear on all your devices when you sign in with the same Google account. This works whether you use a sidebar extension or the built-in bookmark manager.

To make sure sync is enabled, click your profile picture in the top right of Chrome, then look for the sync settings. Make sure "Bookmarks" is turned on in the sync settings. Once enabled, any bookmarks you add or organize on one device will appear on your other devices within a few moments.

Some sidebar extensions offer their own sync functionality independent of Chrome sync. This can be useful if you want to keep certain bookmarks separate from your main Chrome sync or if you use multiple browsers and want all your bookmarks in one place.

## Making the Most of Your Bookmarks

Now that you have your sidebar or extension set up, a few habits will help you get the most out of your bookmarks.

Create a simple folder structure that works for you. Most people find that three to five main folders work well, with subfolders for more specific categories. For example, you might have folders for Work, Shopping, Research, and Entertainment, with subfolders inside each for particular projects or interests.

Regularly clean out bookmarks you no longer need. A cluttered bookmark collection is harder to navigate and less useful than a curated one. Once a month, go through your bookmarks and remove anything that no longer serves you.

Use the search feature in your sidebar extension. When you have accumulated many bookmarks, typing a few letters in the search box is often faster than scrolling through folders to find what you need.

## Wrapping Up

Getting a bookmarks sidebar in Chrome requires a small setup step, but the convenience it provides makes it worth the effort. Whether you choose a simple extension or a more feature-rich option, having your bookmarks visible in a sidebar saves time and keeps your favorite sites within easy reach.

Give a bookmark sidebar extension a try for a week and see how it changes your browsing workflow. You might find that it becomes one of your most-used browser features.
