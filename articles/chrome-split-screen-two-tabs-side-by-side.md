# Chrome Split Screen Two Tabs Side by Side

Chrome split screen two tabs side by side is one of the most useful productivity features that Chrome offers but that many people do not know exists. Whether you are comparing two web pages, copying information between documents, or simply want to see more than one page at a time, splitting your Chrome tabs into a side-by-side view can dramatically improve your browsing experience.

## Why Split Your Screen in Chrome

The main reason people want to split their Chrome tabs is simple: you need to see two things at once. Maybe you are shopping and comparing prices between two different websites. Perhaps you are researching and need to read one article while taking notes from another. Or maybe you are working and need to reference one document while filling out information in another.

When you try to do these things with normal Chrome tabs, you end up constantly clicking back and forth between them. This breaks your concentration and makes simple tasks take much longer than they should. Splitting your screen so you can see two Chrome tabs side by side solves this problem completely. You get both pages visible at the same time, which means you can work faster and with fewer mistakes.

Another reason to use split screen is that Chrome tabs can get lost in a sea of open windows. When you have dozens of tabs open, switching between them becomes a chore. But when you arrange two tabs side by side, each one gets half your screen, making it easy to see and work with both without searching for them in your tab bar.

## How to Use Chrome Built-in Split Screen

Chrome has a built-in way to arrange windows side by side, though it is not immediately obvious. Here is how to do it.

First, click and hold on the tab you want to move. Then, while holding the mouse button, drag the tab down toward the bottom of the window. You will see a dark strip appear at the bottom of your screen. Drop the tab there, and Chrome will create a split view with your current page on top and the dropped tab on the bottom. You can adjust the division between the two by clicking and dragging the divider line up or down.

This method works for splitting vertically (one above the other), but what about side by side (left and right)? For that, you need to use a different approach.

To get true side-by-side split screen, you actually need to arrange your Chrome windows themselves. Click and hold on the Chrome icon in your taskbar or the top of the window, then drag it to the left or right edge of your screen. When you see a shaded area appear, release your mouse, and Chrome will snap to fill half of your screen. Then open your second Chrome window and drag it to the opposite side of your screen. Now you have two Chrome windows side by side, each showing a different tab.

This approach gives you the classic split screen experience where one page is on the left and another is on the right. You can resize the windows by dragging the divider between them.

## Using Chrome Extensions for Split Screen

If you want more flexibility than the built-in methods provide, Chrome extensions are the answer. There are several excellent extensions in the Chrome Web Store that make split screen easy.

One popular option is called Tab Suspender Pro. This extension does more than just split your screen. It helps you manage your tabs intelligently by suspending tabs you are not using to save memory, while keeping your split screen setup easy to access. When you need to compare two pages, you can quickly arrange them side by side and then suspend the ones you are not looking at to keep Chrome running smoothly. Tab Suspender Pro is especially useful if you tend to have many tabs open because it prevents the memory buildup that can happen when you have several pages side by side.

Another good extension is called Split Screen. This one does exactly what its name suggests. Once installed, you simply right-click on a tab and choose "Split to Left" or "Split to Right," and the extension arranges your tabs in a split view instantly. You can also adjust the split ratio to give one side more space than the other.

Tile Tabs is another extension worth mentioning. It lets you split your tabs in various ways, not just two at a time. You can create a grid of four tabs, arrange them in columns or rows, and even save your layouts for later use. This is particularly helpful if you need to compare more than two pages at once.

## Tips for Effective Split Screen Use

Now that you know how to set up split screen, here are some tips to get the most out of it.

Keep your split screen organized. Decide which page goes on which side and stick with it. For example, always keep the page you are reading on the left and the page you are referencing on the right. This consistency makes your workflow feel natural after a short while.

Use keyboard shortcuts to speed things up. Most split screen extensions let you assign keyboard shortcuts for common actions. Learning these shortcuts means you can set up a split view in just a couple of keystrokes.

Consider your screen size. Split screen works best on monitors that are at least 1920 pixels wide. If you have a smaller laptop screen, the text in each split pane might be too small to read comfortably. In that case, you might prefer to just switch between tabs quickly rather than trying to split your already limited screen space.

Close tabs you are not using. One of the benefits of split screen is that it encourages you to focus on just two things at a time. Resist the temptation to keep other tabs open in the background. This not only keeps Chrome running faster but also helps you concentrate on the task at hand.

## When Split Screen Is Most Helpful

Split screen is useful in many situations, but there are a few where it really shines.

Online shopping becomes much easier when you can see two product pages at once. Compare prices, read reviews, and check specifications side by side without clicking back and forth.

Research projects benefit greatly from split screen. Keep your main source open on one side and your notes or another reference document on the other. This makes it much easier to extract the information you need.

Email and calendar management works well with split screen. Keep your inbox on one side and your calendar on the other to stay organized throughout your workday.

Learning online becomes more efficient when you can watch a video tutorial on one side of your screen while following along in an interactive exercise on the other side.

## Give It a Try

Chrome split screen two tabs side by side is one of those features that once you start using, you wonder how you ever lived without it. Whether you use the built-in window arrangement method or a helpful extension like Tab Suspender Pro, seeing two pages at once will make your browsing more productive and less frustrating.

Try setting up a split screen today. Pick two pages you need to use together, arrange them side by side, and see how much easier your work becomes. You might find that this simple change transforms the way you use Chrome entirely.
