# Chrome Surfshark Extension Review 2026

If you have been looking for a chrome surfshark extension review 2026, you are probably trying to decide whether Surfshark's Chrome VPN extension is worth your time and money. With so many VPN options available for Chrome, it can be hard to know which one actually delivers on its promises. This review will walk you through everything you need to know about Surfshark's Chrome extension, including what it does well, where it falls short, and how it compares to other options on the market.

## What Surfshark Chrome Extension Offers

Surfshark is best known as a full VPN service, but they also offer a lightweight Chrome extension that works directly in your browser. The extension is designed to provide a quick and easy way to protect your browsing privacy without installing the full VPN software on your computer.

The Chrome extension uses a proxy-based system rather than a full VPN tunnel. This means it routes your browser traffic through Surfshark's servers to mask your IP address and encrypt your data, but it only affects your Chrome browser rather than your entire internet connection. For many users, this is actually a convenient approach since you might only need VPN protection while browsing the web.

One of the standout features of Surfshark's Chrome extension is the clean interface. Setting it up takes just a few minutes, and you can connect to a server with a single click. The extension shows you your current IP address, the location you appear to be browsing from, and whether your connection is protected.

## Speed and Performance

When evaluating any VPN or proxy extension, speed is always a concern. In our testing for this chrome surfshark extension review 2026, we found that the extension maintains reasonably fast connection speeds for most everyday activities. Streaming video, browsing websites, and even video calls worked without significant lag when connected to nearby servers.

However, when connecting to servers far from your actual location, you will notice some speed reduction. This is typical of any VPN or proxy service since your data has to travel further to reach the server. The amount of speed loss varies depending on the server location and the current network load. If you need the fastest possible speeds for large downloads or latency-sensitive activities, you might want to connect to servers closer to your physical location.

Surfshark maintains a large network of servers across many countries, which helps ensure you can usually find a fast connection nearby regardless of where you are in the world.

## Privacy and Security Features

Privacy is the primary reason most people use a VPN extension, and this is where Surfshark puts in a solid effort. The Chrome extension includes several features designed to enhance your privacy while browsing.

The extension blocks trackers and malicious websites according to Surfshark's claims. It also offers a clean web feature that removes ads and banners, which can make websites load faster and reduce the amount of data you use. These are nice extras that add value beyond just hiding your IP address.

The company is based in the Netherlands, which is part of the Nine Eyes intelligence alliance. While this might concern some users, Surfshark has a no-logs policy, meaning they do not keep records of your browsing activity. They have also undergone independent audits to verify their privacy claims, which adds credibility to their promises.

It is worth noting that the Chrome extension is a proxy rather than a full VPN. This means it only protects your Chrome browser traffic. Other applications on your computer that use the internet will not be protected by the extension. If you need complete protection for all your internet traffic, you would need to use the full Surfshark VPN application instead.

## Ease of Use and Setup

One of the advantages of Surfshark's Chrome extension is how easy it is to get started. You do not need to download any additional software. Simply visit the Chrome Web Store, install the extension, and log in with your Surfshark account. The entire process takes just a few minutes.

The interface is straightforward and beginner-friendly. The main screen shows your connection status, and you can select from a list of server locations. The extension remembers your last used server, so you can connect with one click on future visits.

If you run into any issues, Surfshark offers customer support through live chat and email. Their support team can help with setup questions or troubleshoot any problems you might encounter.

## What Could Be Improved

No product is perfect, and there are a few areas where Surfshark's Chrome extension could improve. The most notable limitation is that it only works in Chrome. If you use other browsers like Firefox, Safari, or Edge, you would need to use a different solution or install the full VPN application.

Some users have reported occasional connection drops, though this seems to vary depending on the server and network conditions. Having an auto-connect feature that automatically reconnects when the connection drops would be a useful addition.

Another consideration is that the proxy-based approach, while convenient, does not provide the same level of security as a full VPN tunnel. For most casual browsing, this is not a significant concern, but users with high security requirements might prefer the full VPN application.

## Comparing to Other Options

The Chrome Web Store has many VPN and proxy extensions available, ranging from free options to premium paid services. Surfshark sits in the mid-to-premium price range. While you can find free alternatives, they often come with limitations such as data caps, slower speeds, or concerns about how they make money.

When compared to other paid VPN Chrome extensions, Surfshark offers competitive features at a reasonable price. The ability to use the extension without installing the full VPN software is a nice middle ground for users who do not need protection for their entire system.

## Is It Worth Installing

For most users looking for browser-based privacy protection, Surfshark's Chrome extension is a solid choice. It is easy to use, reasonably priced, and provides the basic privacy features that most people need when browsing the web.

If you primarily need protection while browsing and do not want to install full VPN software, this extension is definitely worth trying. It works well for everyday privacy needs, streaming content from different regions, and protecting your connection on public WiFi networks.

For users with more advanced requirements, such as protection for all applications or advanced security features, the full Surfshark VPN application might be a better fit. You can always start with the Chrome extension and upgrade later if you find you need more comprehensive protection.
