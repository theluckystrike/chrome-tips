## Key Differences

### Suspension vs Removal Strategy

**Tab Suspender Pro** keeps your tabs visible but unloaded, maintaining your browser's visual state while freeing memory. Suspended tabs show a gray favicon and reload instantly when clicked. You never lose your place or accidentally close important tabs.

Better OneTab removes tabs completely, storing them in a centralized list. This approach saves more memory but breaks your visual workflow. Tabs disappear from your tab bar entirely, requiring you to remember what you had open and manually restore sessions.

### Automation Level

Tab Suspender Pro runs automatically based on time thresholds or memory pressure. Set it to suspend tabs after 30 minutes of inactivity, and forget about manual management. The extension handles everything behind the scenes while you work.

Better OneTab requires manual intervention for every action. You must click the extension icon, select tabs to group, and manually restore them later. This gives you complete control but demands constant attention to tab management.

### Workflow Integration

**Tab Suspender Pro** integrates smoothly with existing browsing habits. Your tabs stay where you left them, just unloaded. Keyboard shortcuts like Ctrl+Tab work normally, and you can see all your tabs at a glance without changing how you navigate.

Better OneTab fundamentally changes how you interact with tabs. Instead of a tab bar, you work with session lists and groups. This appeals to users who prefer project-based organization but requires learning new habits and navigation patterns.

### Performance Impact

The 185KiB size of Tab Suspender Pro means minimal overhead on Chrome startup and operation. Suspension happens instantly without noticeable lag, and the extension uses virtually no background resources when idle.

Better OneTab's 2.16MiB footprint includes extensive UI components for list management, export features, and visual customization. While still lightweight compared to productivity suites, it uses 12x more storage space and slightly more background processing power.

## When to Choose Each

Choose **Tab Suspender Pro** if:
- You want automatic memory management without changing your workflow
- You frequently switch between many tabs and need visual consistency  
- You prefer browser extensions that work invisibly in the background
- You value fast tab restoration over advanced organization features

Choose **Better OneTab** if:
- You work in distinct projects requiring separate tab groups
- You need to export or share tab collections with others
- You want maximum memory recovery even if it means manual management
- You prefer explicit control over automatic background processes

## When Tab Suspender Pro Isn't Enough

Power users managing 100+ tabs across multiple projects may find Tab Suspender Pro's basic grouping insufficient. Research workflows requiring complex tag systems, hierarchical organization, or advanced search capabilities need Better OneTab's robust list management features.

Heavy multitaskers who regularly save and restore entire browsing sessions will appreciate Better OneTab's export functionality. Tab Suspender Pro lacks session backup options, making it unsuitable for users who need to recreate specific tab configurations across devices or share research collections with colleagues.

Users on severely memory-constrained systems might prefer Better OneTab's complete tab removal over Tab Suspender Pro's suspension approach. While suspension saves 60-80% of memory, removal achieves 90%+ savings critical for older machines or complex development environments.
